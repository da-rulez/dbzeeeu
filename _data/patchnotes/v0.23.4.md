## v0.23.4: Gathering power

**New**
- Charge-up effects. Winding up a technique now gathers visible energy in the caster's hands: Kamehameha builds a cyan ball with rays and inflowing sparks before the beam fires, Big Bang Attack fuses cyan and gold, Spirit Bomb pulls motes in from far around, Death Ball boils with purple crackle, and the charged ki blast glows gold. The ball grows with the charge, flares when it is ready, and bursts on release.
- Lockers. A breakable object can now open instead of shattering: shoot a locker and its door swings open with coins dropping out of the front.

**Fixes**
- Loot from a broken object bursts out the moment it breaks instead of a beat later, and always draws in front of the object it fell out of.
- Objects that break or open now show their broken state to everyone nearby, not only the player who broke them.
- Hit rattle on breakable objects reads properly again.

**Map**
- Bloom HQ, first chamber: new tiles and layout.

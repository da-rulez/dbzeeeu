## v0.19.43: Spread and Guard [Patch]

Two techniques for anyone still early on, and a fix for the premium cosmetics.

### Scattering Bullet
- **Five bolts thrown wide in one motion.** They leave your hand scattered across the arc, then bend back in and converge on your target.
- Each one lands lighter than a clean shot, but five of them add up. Scales off Spirit Attack.
- Needs a target selected, like every homing technique.

### Barrier
- **A shell of ki, thrown up on the spot.** No wind-up, five second cooldown.
- **The first 0.6 seconds throw projectiles straight back off it.** Time it on the shot and it costs you nothing.
- **The full 1.2 seconds halve everything that lands.** Read it late and the guard is still there.

### Fixes
- **The premium wings and the Great Ape Helmet are properly account bound again.** All four wing cosmetics could be traded away despite being sold as bound to you. They cannot now.
- The Great Ape Bucket is now the Great Ape Helmet.

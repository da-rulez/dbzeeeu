## v0.17.0: Minigames and the Dragon Keys Store [Release]

The biggest content drop yet: organized minigames, a premium cosmetics store, and player housing.

**Minigames (press M)**
Open the new menu, pick an event, and get warped into an instanced arena:
- Free For All, Tournament brackets, King of the Hill, Base Assault, Dragon Ball Hunt, and Sky Race.
- A fresh game kicks off automatically every so often, weighted by how many players are online. You can also host your own and put up a zeni prize pool.
- Your gear, stats, and inventory are perfectly preserved. What happens in a match stays in the match, and winners take home real rewards.

**Premium Store (press U) and Dragon Keys**
Spend Dragon Keys on per-character cosmetics:
- Name colors, charge-aura colors, nameplate frames, on-death and on-spawn effects, and equippable Titles.
- Titles and effects show off next to your name, both in the world and in chat.

**Player Housing**
Buy a home of your own and fill it with brand new premium furniture.

Jump in and see what you can win!

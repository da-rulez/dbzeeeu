## v0.16.49: Test Zone Fixes [Patch]

Round two of playtest fixes for the new map system's test zone.

- Your character (equipment, aura and all) now depth-sorts correctly against walls and trees; previously only the shadow sorted right while the body drew on top of everything.
- Name and health bars now depth-sort with their owner instead of floating above walls and trees.
- Fixed heavy rubberbanding at high ping around the triangle rock: sloped movement colliders are now baked as prediction-safe axis-aligned strips.
- Shots are now blocked per shape instead of per tile: the half wall only stops bullets on its solid half, and it has its own label so it is not mistaken for the low wall.

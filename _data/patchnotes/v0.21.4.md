## v0.21.4: Destroyer's Baggy Pants [Patch]

New dyeable clothing: Destroyer's Baggy Pants, a standardized take on a certain God of Destruction's iconic trousers. New recolor option: Orchid Purple, sitting between Piccolo Purple and Twilight Purple in the picker. Cliffs sitting next to a differently built neighbor no longer show broken, notched terrain along their own straight run.

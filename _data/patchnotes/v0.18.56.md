## v0.18.56: Standing In Front Of Things [Patch]

### Fixes
- **You no longer walk in front of everything.** On Kami's Lookout the player drew over every tree, pillar and spire no matter where you stood, so nothing on the map could ever be in front of you. Depth works there now: walk behind a pillar and you are behind it.
- **Scenery stacks in the right order.** The pillars were drawing over the conifers even though the conifers stand closer to the camera. Objects now sort by where they actually are rather than by what kind of object they are.
- Same fault found and fixed on the First Bloom Chamber, the test house and the loadtest arena while we were in there.

Go stand behind a pillar.

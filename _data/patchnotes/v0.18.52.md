## v0.18.52: Same Price For Everyone [Patch]

### Minigames
- **In an equalize-at-start match, a level costs the same for everyone.** The stats were already equalized, but the orb price of the next level was still being read off your real level, so a veteran was buying their 101st level while their opponent bought their 2nd. Same gain in the ring, hundreds of times the orbs. The rule was quietly punishing you for having progressed, which is the exact opposite of the point.
- Now both fighters buy their 1st, 2nd, 3rd level of the match at the same price, whatever they walked in with.
- **The character window shows your in-match progress**, not your real levels sitting next to base stats. You see the ladder you are actually climbing, at the price you are actually paying.
- None of this touches normal play. Outside a match your real levels and real prices are exactly as before, and everything bought inside a match still reverts when you leave.

Go get it.

## v0.20.50: Second Breath [Patch]

**The healing techniques have their proper names.** Second Breath mends you, Helping Hand mends an ally, Sacred Water mends everyone standing with you, and Dragon Clan Rites is the Namekian discipline that makes all of it land harder and hold longer. The regen each one leaves behind now carries the same name, so the effect on your bar says what put it there.

**Sacred Water looks like its name.** Droplets lift off you in a rising stream, the light around you turns blue, and three rings break outward across the ground as it lands.

**There is a guide now, at [dbzee.eu/wiki.html](https://dbzee.eu/wiki.html)** — how loci, crafting, infusion, weather, homes, guilds, the Dragon Balls and the minigames actually work, every technique the Collection Log names, and a full run-down of the settings and key bindings. Nothing you are meant to find in game is spoiled there.

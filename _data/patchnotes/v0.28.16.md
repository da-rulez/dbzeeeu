## v0.28.16: Golden Dragon Fist [Patch]

- New legendary technique: **Golden Dragon Fist**. Charge for 1.8 seconds while gold ki builds around you and the ground shakes, then a dragon of golden ki leaves your fist, coils once around you and climbs. It slows at the top of its arc, then dives onto your selected target faster and faster, and bursts over the area: heavy damage, knockback and screen shake. Learned from its technique scroll.
- A lobbed technique that lands right on its target now knocks it back too. Before, a direct hit had no push at all.

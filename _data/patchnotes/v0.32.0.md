## v0.32.0: A World That Reacts [Release]

Fights and weather now leave their mark on the ground. All of it is cosmetic and can be turned down or off under Options, Graphics, World effects.

**The ground**
- Blasts leave craters with a ring of thrown-up earth. Craters that overlap join into one. They stop at cliff edges, and rubble falls to the foot.
- Charging or transforming pushes dust outward and lifts bits of whatever you stand on: grass, dirt, sand, snow or rock.
- Walking leaves footprints in sand, snow and, lightly, in dirt. Sprinting glides and leaves a trail, not prints.
- Jumping and landing kick up the ground, differently on each surface.

**Snow, rain and water**
- Snow settles in patches in cold weather. Steps, beams, blasts and charges clear it, and walking the same way again and again treads a path.
- Rain leaves puddles. Stepping or landing in one splashes, and a blast empties it.
- Hits on water splash. A low beam leaves a wake and steam.

**Trees and air**
- Woods have fallen leaves. Blasts and tremors shake more out of the trees, or snow out of snowy pines. Leaves get kicked, blown and burnt.
- Gusts of wind carry leaves, sand or snow. At night there are fireflies among the trees.

**Projectiles**
- Spirit Bomb is one ball from charge to throw. It hits four times and splashes on the target it lands on.
- Balls fly at a height that fits their size, follow the ground over cliffs and steps, and cast a shadow.
- Piercing shots hit again while they pass through. A splash lands every time and carries the shot's effects.
- Spirit Bomb and Death Ball fly for up to 20 seconds, with a smaller splash.

**Fixes**
- A spell's burst effect is no longer drawn through a cliff beside the caster.

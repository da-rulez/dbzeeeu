## v0.19.72: Item Infusion [Feature]

- **Equipment can be infused.** Bring a piece to an Infusion Bench, pay the materials, and pick one of up to three upgrades. Each tier asks for more, and gives more.
- **Every copy is its own item.** Your gi and someone else's gi with the same name can hold completely different rolls. The infusion travels with the piece through trade, the bank and the ground, and colours you picked stay exactly as they were.
- **The value is rolled at the bench.** Options show a range, never a promise, and the roll happens when you press Infuse.
- **Tier shows on the icon.** One star per tier, up to a dragon at T5, so a bag full of gear says what it is at a glance.
- **New materials:** Bloomstone, Sporeite, Thornite and Verdite.

Three pieces can be infused to start with: the Turtle School Gi (5 tiers), Solum Bloom Pacers (3) and the Recovered Scouter (4).

### Also in this patch

- Item panels read straighter: numbers are bold and right-aligned so they stack, vitals wear their own colours, and gains and losses are green and red everywhere.
- An infused stat shows how much of it came from the bench, in brackets after the name. Effects gained from infusing sit under the item's own, in their own colour.
- Every number in the UI is a whole number now.
- Icons carry your recolour and tier frame in every window, not just the bag.

## v0.25.2: Doorways, and odds on a shot [Patch]

Stand in a doorway and the wall is in front of you again, the way it looks.

### Fixed
- **Standing inside a wall.** In a doorway, under an overhang or anywhere a wall's own art is beneath your feet, you now always render behind it. It used to depend on where inside that tile you stood, down to a pixel, so a step could put you on top of the wall you were standing in.

### New
- **Odds on a pierce or a bounce.** A technique can pierce or ricochet only some of the time. Each target it passes through, or each thing it comes off, rolls for it. The technique's tooltip says so: "Pierces 3 targets, 60% chance", "Bounces 3 times, 75% chance". A technique that always does it reads exactly as before.

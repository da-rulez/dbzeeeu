## v0.18.61: Kame Island [Patch]

### Tournaments
- **A second tournament arena: Kame Island.** Tournaments now roll between Kami's Palace and a palm-covered island with the Kame House on it. Fighters square off on the open grass, the rest of the bracket waits on the south shore, and the eliminated watch from the east side.
- The sea is solid, so nobody wanders off the beach mid-bout.

### Weather
- **Kame Island has its own weather.** Mostly bright tropical sun, with the occasional thick downpour that blows through in under a minute and clears again. The rain darkens the island and comes in off the sea at an angle while it lasts.

Bring a towel.

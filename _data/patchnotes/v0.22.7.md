## v0.22.7: Night Light [Patch]

- Auras light the ground at night in their own shape. A flame aura throws a flickering flame-shaped glow, a build-up that swells the aura swells the light with it, and a bigger form is a brighter light. Daylight is untouched.
- Nights are darker. The darkest hour dropped to about a third of daylight, so lamps and auras read as light again.
- Shadows fade with the light. They used to switch on at a fixed dawn hour and off at a fixed dusk hour; they now follow the brightness of the sky itself, growing in with the first grey and out with the last.
- Sprint afterimages spawn much fainter and fade out longer. They were drawn at full opacity and read as a second body.
- Reverting a transformation after a world change (warp, house, dimension) left its buff, icon and stats behind. The form is now stripped properly.
- Charged shots no longer flash off to one side for a frame: the gathering ball is placed the moment it appears, and the fired comet no longer measures its first stretch from the map corner.
- The small crystal stones on the grass no longer block walking.

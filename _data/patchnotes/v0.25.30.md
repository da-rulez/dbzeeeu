## v0.25.30: Capes, Hair and Portraits [Patch]

### Changed
- **Capes sit on your shoulders.** The Great Saiyaman Cape and the Namekian Cape now drape over your clothes at the shoulder: clasps, shoulder pads and collar go on top of your gi or armour, and the cape still hangs behind you. Works in every pose, including turned ones.
- **Hair in front stays in front.** Bangs, the locks that frame your face and hair falling over your shoulders now draw above your gear. Shades and scouters sit under your bangs, and from the side long hair falls over your cape. Beards sit under your bangs. This covers every hairstyle, in every colour and transformation.
- **Portraits match the game.** Character select, party and guild portraits, minigame rosters, tournament brackets and leaderboards now draw your character exactly as the world does: same layering, your dye colours, your beard and your tail.
- **Dressed NPCs keep their look in dialogue.** An NPC wearing hair or an outfit shows it in the dialogue portrait too.

### Fixed
- The Moss Namek skin had no meditate pose. It has one now.

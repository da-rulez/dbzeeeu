## v0.18.93: Things In Their Right Place [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Techniques no longer fly in from the middle of the screen.** Impact rings, gathered orbs and damage numbers appeared at the centre of the view for a frame and then shot across to where they belonged. They now appear where they happen.
- **Thrown spheres sort against the world properly again.** A Spirit Bomb in flight drew on top of trees, characters and everything else no matter where it actually was. It now passes behind what it is behind, and in front of what it is in front of.
- **A sphere gathered overhead is no longer hidden by overhangs.** While charging it is held above your head, so it now draws above treetops and roofs instead of disappearing under them. Once thrown it sorts normally again.
- **Your facing no longer sticks after leaving the window.** Holding the aim key and then alt-tabbing left the game believing the key was still down, so afterwards walking in a new direction would snap you back to the last direction you aimed.

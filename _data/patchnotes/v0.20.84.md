## v0.20.84: The Kanassans [Content]

**Four fish-people out of Kanassa**, a step up from the rock golems and every bit as unfriendly. The Wader closes and shoves. The Diviner leads its shots, because Kanassan sight is not a rumour. The Bloodfin arrives already at arm's length. The Tidespeaker freezes the ground you were about to use, then opens a lane down it.

**They carry their own drops:** shells and bloom water off the common ones, and a seer's keeping off the diviners, up to a Kanassan geode or a cut sapphire. The Kanassan stones have moved off the sapphire golem, which now drops blue stone of its own.

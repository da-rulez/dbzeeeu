## v0.18.46 — Say It Out Loud [Patch]

Warnings you can actually see, a cast bar for every caster, and one less bar cluttering your character.

### Interface
- **Warnings now appear over your character, in colour.** Refusals like "Can't warp while in combat!", "Not enough Ki!" or "Inventory is full!" only ever went to the chat log — so with chat closed, the game looked broken rather than like it was answering you. They now float up off your character and fade. Everything still goes to chat as well, so nothing is lost.
- **They can't spam you.** At most one popup per second, and repeating the same warning is suppressed for four seconds — mashing a blocked action gives you one message, not a column of them.
- **You can turn them off.** Settings → Gameplay → *Overhead Warning Popups*. The chat copy keeps working.
- **The stamina bar is gone from above your character.** It is already on your HUD, and it was the main thing crowding the space.

### Combat
- **Cast bars.** Any spell with a cast time now shows a white bar filling as it winds up — for players *and* NPCs, so a slow enemy attack is something you can see coming and step away from. It sits in the slot the stamina bar used to occupy, so your character is no more cluttered than before.

See you in the arena.

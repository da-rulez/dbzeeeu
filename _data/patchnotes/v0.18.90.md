## v0.18.90: Wings [Patch]

### New: Outfits in the Z-Shop
- **A new Outfits tab**, showing each piece on your own character rather than as a flat icon.
- **Four sets of wings**, Back slot, purely cosmetic with no stat bonuses at all:
  - **Ember Drake Wings** (900)
  - **Eternal Dragon Wings** (1200)
  - **Obsidian Drake Wings** (1500)
  - **Golden Wish Wings** (2000)
- Outfits are **bound to you**: they cannot be traded, banked, dropped or sold, and they never drop on death. You can delete one, and if you do you can **claim it back for free** from the shop, so a mis-click costs you nothing. Only ever one of each.
- The Z-Shop is closed inside minigames and dimensions.

### Transformations
- **Super Saiyan 4 and Super Saiyan 5 are now Ultimate Power skins for Hair 3.**

### Fixes
- Back-slot items (wings, capes) now render behind your hair when facing the camera instead of over it.

Wear them. They do nothing. That is the point.

## v0.25.10: IP bans on the dashboard [Patch]

### Added
- **IP bans.** Staff can ban an address from the admin dashboard (new IP Bans tab, and a Ban IP button on a player's page next to the account ban). A banned address cannot log in or register, and every account last seen from it is logged out and kicked. Bans can be timed or permanent and are listed with the accounts seen from that address.

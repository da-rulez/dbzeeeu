## v0.18.72: Nudge [Patch]

### Under the hood
- **Arrow keys move the selected object properly now.** The first press worked and then the selection appeared to drop, because the arrow key was also being read as menu navigation and quietly took focus off the map. It keeps focus now, so you can tap repeatedly or hold a direction, and a held run undoes in one go rather than one step per repeat. The step follows the snap setting: one pixel, quarter tile, half tile or a full tile.

Nothing changes in-game with this one.

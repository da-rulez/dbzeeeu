## v0.16.45: NPC Power Levels [Patch]

Size up your enemies before you swing.

**Scouting at a glance**
- NPCs now show their Power Level in brackets next to their name.
- The number is color coded against YOUR own Power Level, in 8 shades: green means weaker than you, yellow means an even match, and the scale climbs through amber and orange into deep red for enemies far above you.
- The colors update as you grow, so yesterday's red boss slowly fades toward green as you spend orbs.

**First wave of ratings**
- The Practice Dummy, Calibration drones, the Calibration Overseer and General White all have Power Levels assigned. More NPCs will get their ratings in upcoming patches.

**For the curious**
- Friendly villagers keep their secrets: NPCs without an assigned Power Level simply show no bracket.

## v0.18.95: Down To Earth [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Ground techniques now start at your feet.** A lane technique was anchored at chest height, so the seam and its telegraph both floated above the ground they were supposed to be tearing open. They now sit on the floor and start just ahead of you, the same way a thrown technique leaves your hand rather than your chest.
- Fissure Wave's eruptions were tuned to sit inside the damaged strip rather than overhang it.

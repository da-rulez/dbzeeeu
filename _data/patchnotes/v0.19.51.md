## v0.19.51: Hand It Over [Patch]

### Rarity
- **Items have a rarity now**: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, and Z. Each has its own colour, and the better ones sit on a lit backing wherever the icon is drawn, so a good drop is obvious at a glance in a full bag.
- Rarity is a reading of an item, never a stat. It changes nothing about what the item does.

### Receiving items
- **You get to see what you were handed.** Where an event chooses to, your character strikes a pose and the item hangs above your head on its rarity colour, long enough to read.
- Given several things at once, they come one at a time rather than piling up.

### For content
- Animations can hold a single frame longer than the rest, so a pose can rest instead of flicking past.
- The animation editor plays the actual sprite now, rather than highlighting cells on the sheet.
- Events can play an animation on the player or on an NPC, instead of only pinning one frame.

## v0.20.52: Enemies Can Actually Fire Those [Patch]

**Enemy beams and charged attacks now exist.** Any enemy whose attack was a beam or a wind-up shot has been playing the animation and firing nothing at all — no projectile, no damage — for as long as those attacks have been in the game. The Calibration Overseer's Ki Beam is the one you are most likely to have met. They land now.

They were being looked up as if they were players, and an enemy is not one, so the whole attack was quietly dropped on the floor.

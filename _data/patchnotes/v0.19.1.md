## v0.19.1: Cliff Corners [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Cliff corners are built properly again.** The bottom corners of a cliff are two tiles tall, and only the lower one was being placed: the upper piece was instead sprinkled at random points along straight cliff edges, where it did not belong. Corners now stack correctly whether the drop is a single step or a full wall, and long edges read cleanly along their whole length.

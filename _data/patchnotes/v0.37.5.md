## v0.37.5: Trial Peak fixes [Hotfix]

- Dying on Trial Peak now brings you back on Trial Peak, next to the Playtest Guide. Before, it tried to send you to Solum Bloom HQ, and in the browser that could freeze the game.
- The creatures on Trial Peak hit much softer (about a third of before) and have half their health. The MOBA creeps are easier too.
- The Playtest Guide now also gives you 10,000 orbs, once. Press C to spend them on your stats.

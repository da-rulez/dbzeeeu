## v0.19.45: Read the Arc [Patch]

Thunder Break now tells you where it lands before it lands, and drain effects finally drain.

### Thunder Break
- **It winds up now.** Yellow current builds around you for a beat before the sweep, so it can be seen coming and it can be interrupted.
- **The tell is the exact arc it hits.** Cone techniques telegraph as the cone, out to the same reach, filling outward the way the hit does. No more guessing from a rectangle.

### Drain effects
- **Electrified and Kaioken now actually take stamina off you.** Both were fighting your natural recovery and roughly breaking even; the drain now outpaces it.
- **Frost drains stamina instead of freezing recovery**, for the same reason.
- Vitals no longer sit pinned at full while something is draining them, and cannot be pushed below zero by a drain.

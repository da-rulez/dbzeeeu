## v0.25.31: Tighter, Lighter, Safer [Patch]

### Changed
- **Movement answers faster.** The server no longer lets a backlog of your inputs pile up behind a jittery connection (every queued step was 33 ms of extra delay that the ping number never showed), and every other player and NPC you see is now one tick fresher.
- **Less data on the wire.** NPC health rides the regular position packet, other players' vitals go out as a small packed packet only when their bar changes, a technique's look is sent once per fight instead of once per shot, and orb pulls are batched. In an eight-player test the server sent about a quarter less data.
- **A lighter server tick.** NPC crowd handling, the save sweep and several scans were rewritten. In the same test the physics peak dropped from 6 to 13 ms to 3 to 5 ms and NPC work fell by about 70%.
- **A lighter client frame.** Characters without an aura no longer render a hidden silhouette every frame, off-screen NPCs do less, and technique lights are capped.
- **Passwords.** New and reset passwords need at least 8 characters. Remember-me no longer keeps your password readable on disk.

### Fixed
- Bouncing techniques bounce off walls on every map again.
- Bots now count on the hill and can damage MOBA bases.
- Pressing Escape to close a window no longer also opens the options menu or clears your target.
- The shield bar no longer disappears when you take a hit.
- Teleports and respawns no longer smear or glide across the screen, for you or for the people watching.
- A countdown that falls back to the lobby lets everyone move again.
- The chat window no longer grows without limit while open.
- Several ways to end up locked out with "already logged in" after a bad connection are closed.
- Closed several ways to duplicate Zeni or items. Saves can no longer be overwritten by an older copy.

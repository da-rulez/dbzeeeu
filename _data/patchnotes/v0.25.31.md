## v0.25.31: Boss Bars, Tighter, Lighter, Safer [Patch]

### Added
- **Boss HP bar.** Fighting a boss puts a plate at the top of the screen: portrait, name, power level, the boss's active effects and a layered HP bar. Big bosses carry several layers in different colours, drained one after the other with the next showing through underneath; the chips under the bar count what is left and shatter as layers go, and a hit through several layers flashes each of them past. It fills up when the fight starts, shows for you, your party and your guild mates nearby, fades when you walk away or warp out, and empties when the boss falls. Options > Interface > Boss bar turns it off.

### Changed
- **Movement answers faster.** The server no longer lets a backlog of your inputs pile up behind a jittery connection, and everyone you see is a tick fresher.
- **Less data on the wire** and **a lighter server tick**: about a quarter less traffic, NPC work down 70%, physics peaks halved in an eight-player test.
- **A lighter client frame.** Hidden silhouettes, off-screen NPCs and technique lights all cost less.
- **Passwords** need at least 8 characters; remember-me no longer keeps your password readable on disk.

### Fixed
- Bouncing techniques bounce off walls on every map again.
- Bots count on the hill and can damage MOBA bases.
- Escape no longer also opens the options menu or clears your target.
- The shield bar no longer disappears when you take a hit.
- Teleports and respawns no longer smear across the screen.
- A countdown that falls back to the lobby lets everyone move again.
- The chat window no longer grows without limit while open.
- Several "already logged in" lockouts after a bad connection are closed.
- Closed several ways to duplicate Zeni or items; saves can no longer be overwritten by an older copy.

## v0.20.25: Sound, Charge and Beam Pass [Patch]

**Every technique makes a noise now**, Barrier and the rest included, and each one has its own fixed sound instead of a different take every time you press it.

- **Held charges** hum while you hold them and crack when you let go, instead of announcing a shot that had not happened yet.
- **Lobbed shards** were completely silent. A Sunburst Volley now sounds like ten shards, a volley like one throw.
- **The big held shots** whistle while they fly and land with a heavier explosion.
- Mighty Impact carries the wind, Explosive Wave the blast, Thunder Break's fist its crackle.

**Ki Beam** is a beam again: the chunks come out closer together instead of beading, and its glare is down to something you can see past. Its charge-up is gold now rather than blue, and both the charge and the shots sit where they should, left and right.

**Big Bang Attack** is a lighter blue that matches the ball you charge, and it now gathers with rings sweeping the floor beneath you.

**Your aura colour is your charge colour.** The rings and the glow follow the aura you have equipped, and they keep it while transformed instead of mixing with the transformation's own colour.

**Inner Focus** shows something happening: a white swell with rings.

**Fixes.** Reverting when you are not transformed does nothing at all now, instead of playing the whole animation. Ki cost reduction finally applies to held techniques, so a beam held with full reduction is free. The warning text over your head no longer flashes in the middle of the screen before it arrives.

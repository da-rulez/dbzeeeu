## v0.18.48 — Titles, Placed [Patch]

### Titles
- **A title can now read either before or after your name.** Each one is authored as a rank that sits in front — *Admin Kakarot* — or as an epithet that follows it — *Kakarot, The Champion*.
- **Epithet titles get their own line above your character.** Instead of being crammed onto the end of your name, they now appear on a second line directly beneath it, so long titles no longer stretch your nameplate across half the screen. In chat they stay on a single line.
- Every title that already exists keeps the placement it has now, so nothing you have equipped changes.

See you in the arena.

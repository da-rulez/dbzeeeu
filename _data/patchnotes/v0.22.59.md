## v0.22.59 — Drawn projectiles are back, and the disc grinds [Hotfix]

**Fix**
- Every drawn-orb projectile stopped rendering in 0.22.58 — the Destructo Disc, Charged Ki Blast, Spirit Bomb and Death Ball all fired invisibly. Restored.

**Destructo Disc**
- Slower and heavier. It now stays on a target long enough to cut it several times before passing through to the next, rather than clipping each one once.
- Damage per hit lowered to match, so a full charge through a line lands a little less than it did before.

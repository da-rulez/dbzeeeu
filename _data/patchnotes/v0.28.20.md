## v0.28.20: Under the Hood [Patch]

A big stability, fairness and anti-cheat pass across the whole server.

**Changes**
- Conditional passives (Iron Will, Last Stand, Relentless, Untouchable, Zenkai Boost) have a 30s cooldown, shown on the passive slots and in tooltips.
- Silence also interrupts a technique still winding up or charging. Techniques already firing keep going.
- Support techniques always cost Ki and go on cooldown, even when nobody needed healing.
- Other players show only their health bar.
- Minigames can't be joined once the countdown starts, and someone leaving no longer reopens signups.
- After an invite expires or is declined, the same player waits 30s before inviting you again.
- House sale money always goes to your bank, even when you are in a Locus or a match. Bank stacks hold up to 2,147,483,647.

**Fixes**
- The Infusion Bench works again.
- You keep your guild after warping into a house or Locus, and a muted player stays muted.
- Disconnecting while changing maps no longer leaves your party without a leader.
- Dragon Balls on the ground no longer vanish after a minute.
- Monsters no longer go missing, stand around dead, or turn invisible after you warp back.
- Fixed a loading screen that never ended after two quick map changes.
- Leaving a minigame no longer drags you back to the stands.
- Death Ball and Destructo Disc Blade hit repeatedly again.
- Removing one source of an effect no longer removes another source's copy.
- Long sessions no longer slow down from beams and shots.

**Fair play**
- A missed area attack costs Ki and goes on cooldown.
- Shots no longer pass through thin walls, and out-of-range or hidden targets can't be locked.
- Leaving a match and logging off counts as a loss.
- Closed several ways to get an item or reward twice.

**Saving**
- One bad save can no longer stop everyone else's progress from saving.
- A quick relog waits for your last save.

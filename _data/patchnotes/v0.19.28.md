## v0.19.28: Shockwave [Patch]

The ground finally reacts when you charge, and the numbers you hover are the numbers you read.

### Explosive Wave
- **Shockwave rings now actually appear.** They pulse out from under your feet and widen as the charge builds. They were being drawn underneath the floor the whole time, which is why nobody ever saw one.
- **The damage lands with the impact, not with the last frame.** The front now fills the whole radius by the moment the blast reads as hitting, instead of trailing the animation to its end.
- **Explosive Wave has a scroll.** Technique Scroll: Explosive Wave, so you can actually learn it.

### Tooltips read like the menus do
- **Hover tooltips are coloured and formatted now.** KI costs, damage and stat changes carry the same colours in a tooltip as they do in the technique book, instead of showing up as flat grey text.
- **Effects are split by who gets them in tooltips too.** "On Target" above, "On You" below, matching the book.
- **Equipment hover shows gains in green and losses in red** with proper stat names.

### Fixes
- **Your spawn effect no longer replays itself.** Casting anything while it was still playing used to restart it from the top the moment your cast finished. An interrupted spawn effect is simply over now.
- **Being slowed no longer rubberbands you.** The game was still predicting your movement at full speed for a moment after a slow landed, then yanking you back once the server corrected it. Slows now take hold immediately and smoothly.

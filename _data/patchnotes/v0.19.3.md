## v0.19.3: Wave, Not Wall [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Line techniques read as a wave again.** Each burst held its final frame until the whole line had finished, so what should have been an eruption travelling outward ended up as the entire lane lit at once. Bursts now clear as they finish.
- **The eruptions sit where the technique actually hits.** Cast sideways they sat below the damaged strip; cast up or down they were off-centre. Both now line up with the ground being broken.

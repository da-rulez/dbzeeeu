## v0.29.0: Combat Overhaul [Release]

A big pass on how fights play, read and look.

**Combat and balance**
- New damage formula: a hit scales with your attack against the target's defence, so a stat gap matters most at low levels.
- Bigger pools: HP 500 + 60 per level, Ki 300 + 20 per level, base Ki regen 2 per second. Monster hits scale with your HP pool.
- An interrupted technique costs nothing. Top tier techniques cannot be interrupted.
- Beams deal less per hit, Serpent Beam homes less tightly, and finishers take a share of max HP when they land.
- Bleeds are a flat percent of max HP per tick. Sanctuary Ward clears and blocks them.

**Telegraphs**
- A telegraph now shows exactly where a technique can hit, also on cliffs and plateaus.
- Lanes and cones stop at a wall or cliff edge. Bombs warn and hit on every floor they reach, and a volley shows a ring per shard.
- Round areas are ovals everywhere, in the warning and in the hit.

**Monsters and bots**
- Monsters attack one thing at a time, with wind-ups that match the attack and their own sounds.
- MOBA camps attack on sight. Bots farm camps, use items, and fight with their own styles.
- Monsters and bots move with more weight: less jitter, no sliding, no walking on the spot.

**Look and feel**
- Cliffs cast shadows from their outline, and day and night have warmer colours with a darker night.
- Loot, Zeni and orbs fall down a cliff instead of snapping to the ground, and lie clear of the rock.
- Beams leave from the palms. Your own shots stop on the monster they hit, and it flashes right away.
- Lobbed blasts start at the caster's height, fly smoothly and land where the ring says.

**Fixes**
- Bodies are no longer drawn inside a plateau, and team mates no longer spawn on one spot.
- Effects are in the right place from their first frame.

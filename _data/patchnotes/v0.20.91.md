## v0.20.91: Dressed NPCs [Feature]

**NPCs can wear hair and gear now.** Any NPC can be given hair, facial hair and equipment in the editor, drawn with the same paperdoll the players use: same layering, same recolour rules, so a piece dyed Deep Ocean on an NPC looks exactly like the one you would dye yourself.

It is visual only. Nothing worn grants the NPC a stat, and nothing worn drops when it dies. The point is that a soldier in armour is a setting on a soldier, not a whole new enemy.

**Transformations follow the suffix.** A transformed NPC looks for the same sheets a transformed player would, hair and beard included, and quietly keeps its normal art for anything that has no transformed version.

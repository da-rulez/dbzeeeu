## v0.25.22: Props [Patch]

### Changed
- **Breakables are now props.** A prop opens by being hit (lockers, crates, boulders) or by pressing [E] within its own reach (chests, stashes), never both. Props you press open have no health and cannot be targeted.
- **MOBA and arena chests** are props on the new lab crate art: camp stashes, warlord hoards, the overlord prize, the boss chest.
- **Minigame props** live with the match: a chest opened in one match is closed in the next, and what you open never follows you out.
- **Loot lands in front.** Coins and items that pop out of a prop always draw in front of it, whichever way they scatter.
- **Coins fly slower** out of a prop and settle with a gentler hop.
- Content editor: the Props tab gained open mode, interact reach and prompt, and a minigame checkbox. Map editor: the interact point field is now "prop".

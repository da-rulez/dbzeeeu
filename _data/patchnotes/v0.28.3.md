## v0.28.3: Cleaner Beams [Patch]

### Beams
- Beams no longer flicker back behind the one firing them.
- A beam at full length now holds steady at its tip. Only a beam that hits something keeps its head pressed there.
- Let go of a beam and it now thins out and breaks apart as it drains, instead of shrinking solid into its head.
- Beams and shots fly over items on the ground instead of behind them: loot now lies flat on the floor.

### Fixes
- Practice and showcase dummies no longer vanish when they turn or get hit.
- Every beam fired no longer fills the game log with errors.

## v0.18.30: Altar Polish & Reward Fixes [Patch]

A small follow-up patch.

- **Dragon Ball altars:** placing or stealing a ball now shows a small, clean progress bar **right under the pedestal** (with a "Placing" / "Stealing" label) instead of a pop-up window — cleaner, and you can keep your eyes on the action.
- **Dimension rewards:** fixed rewards nested inside a reward group not being granted on claim, and a reward pool being overwritten by your next run of the same Locus (so unclaimed rewards can no longer be lost that way).

See you in the arena!

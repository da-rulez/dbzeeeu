## v0.27.0: Cliff Heights [Release]

### New
- Cliffs have real height. Standing on a plateau draws you over its rock face, and walking under its overhang tucks you behind the top, with only your head showing over the rim near the edge. The same goes for NPCs, objects and projectiles.
- Stacked plateaus each sort and block on their own floor, so you can no longer walk off the side of a cliff that stands on another cliff.
- Low one-high ledges are half a tile tall: they block walking, never shots.
- Stairs change your floor the moment you step onto them, on your own screen too, instead of after a round trip to the server.
- The generated maps (the arenas and the islands) follow the same rules.

### Changed
- Lanes and cones travel: they stop at the first wall or cliff face, and the mark on the ground stops with them. Bursts still spread around corners.
- Ground marks (cast warnings, impact rings) lie under walls and cliff faces like anything else on the ground, and only show on top of a plateau the technique can reach.

### Fixed
- Warping onto a staircase from a higher floor no longer leaves you on the wrong floor.

## v0.27.8: Spells and Cliff Floors [Patch]

### Changed
- Instant Transmission can warp to a target one cliff floor above or below you. It lands behind the target where there is ground to stand on, never inside a rock face.
- Bombs and barrages can be thrown at targets up to two floors below or one floor above, and their blasts hit those floors.
- Projectiles, AoE spells, lanes and area heals hit only the floor you stand on.

### Fixed
- Lanes and cones cast on top of a plateau are no longer cut short by the rock beneath you.

## v0.22.54 — Destructo Disc, stripped back [Patch]

**Destructo Disc**
- The disc is the whole effect now: energy swirls inside it and the rim runs hot, with nothing else around it. The after-images behind it, the coloured ground glow and the gathering wisps are gone.
- It spins up centred above your head instead of out to one side, so it no longer swings around as you turn.
- Both while charging and in flight it casts a plain grey shadow on the ground, the same read your own shadow gives, so you can see how high it is being held.

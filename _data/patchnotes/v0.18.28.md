## v0.18.28: Tournaments, Scoreboards & the Minigame Glow-Up [Patch]

Minigames got a big presentation pass — readable tournament brackets, cleaner scoreboards, real race times, and a scoreboard you can finally move where you want.

**Tournaments**
- The Tournaments tab now lists every tournament — server-run and player-hosted — with a live bracket you can actually read: player portraits, power levels, the bout that's live right now, and who's already through.
- Before it starts you see the sign-up grid filling up; once it's rolling it becomes the full bracket.
- Win it all and you get a proper CHAMPION screen.

**Scoreboards**
- Rebuilt compact and clean. Teams get real names (no more "Team 0"), grouped by color.
- Hold **V** to expand the full player roster.
- Drag the scoreboard wherever you like — grab it by the header and it remembers the spot for your account.

**Racing**
- Your result is now a real time (mm:ss.cc), and the board ranks you by position — fastest across the line up top.

**Fixes & hardening**
- Squashed several furniture duplication and item-loss bugs around placing and moving pieces.
- Tightened minigame isolation so nothing you gain inside a match can follow you back to the overworld, and your gear, spells and stats always come back exactly as they were.

Jump in and check it out — see you in the arena!

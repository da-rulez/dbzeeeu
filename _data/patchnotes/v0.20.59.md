## v0.20.59: Break It Down [Feature]

**Gear you have outgrown is now raw material.** Every piece worth salvaging has a **Break Down** recipe at a crafting table: hand it over with a Zeni fee for the tools and it comes apart into infusion materials. Better gear gives better materials, and the strong pieces carry a chance of something from the tier above on top. Breaking down never fails.

**Recipes can now yield a range, and a bonus.** An output reads as "3-6 Bloomstone" and the number is rolled when the craft lands. Recipes that carry a bonus item show it plainly before you commit: "10% chance: 1-2 Sporeite". A craft that cannot hand over both the output and the bonus will not start, so nothing is ever consumed for a result there is no room for.

**Luck finally does something.** Two things, in fact. It improves a chance by a share of itself, so 10% Luck turns a 10% chance into 11%. And it buys extra rolls on any range and keeps the best one: every whole 100% is one guaranteed extra roll, and the leftover is the chance of one more. At 150% Luck you roll twice, and half the time three times. It applies to what a break-down yields, to whether a bonus lands, and to the value an infusion rolls. The chance printed in the crafting window is already yours, Luck included.

Bloomstone, Sporeite, Thornite and Verdite now wear the rarity frames their value always implied.

The guide has a new **Luck & modifiers** page covering all of it, plus every other quiet stat: Attunement, Control, Tenacity, deflect, absorption and the rest.

## v0.17.2: Minigames Menu and Fixes [Patch]

More polish based on your feedback.

**Minigames menu (press M)**
- New Play and Results tabs. Check your played and won record for each game, browse upcoming and in-progress matches, and Register or Spectate right from the list.
- Fixed the Host button that used to need several clicks before responding.
- Hosting is now for tournaments, with a minimum prize pool to make them worth entering.

**Fixes**
- You now get your entry fee back if you leave a game before it actually starts, including during the countdown.
- Large currency amounts display correctly again (no more "1000K"): full numbers up to 100K, then K, then M and B.
- Custom name colors can no longer be picked too dark to read.

Thanks for the reports. Keep them coming!

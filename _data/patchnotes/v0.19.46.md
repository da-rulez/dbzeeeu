## v0.19.46: Know Your Numbers [Patch]

Recovery is now something you can see, invest in, and have taken off you in two different ways.

### Regen on the character sheet
- **HP, Ki and Stamina regen are listed under Speed**, showing the rate that actually runs after gear, buffs and debuffs. Green when it is climbing, red when something is eating it.

### Two ways to lose recovery
- **A flat decrease takes a fixed amount off your regen**, so building into recovery lets you outpace it. Electrified, Frost and Kaioken all take 10 a second off your stamina regen.
- **A percentage decrease scales with whatever you built**, so it cannot be out-invested. Storms use it: caught out in heavy snow, nothing recovers at all until you find shelter.
- Drains are separate from either. Electrified takes 6 ki and 4 stamina a second on top of what it does to your recovery, and Kaioken burns 4 stamina a second while it is up.

## v0.35.3: Beams that hit what they touch, and bushes you can stand on [Patch]

**Beams**
- A beam hits a body only where it touches it: the feet within a body's depth of the beam's row, the beam's edge within reach of the body's height. A beam fired sideways now flies past someone standing a step north or south of it, and one fired from a ledge passes over heads below.
- Beam heads are round, and no longer bounce back on a hit or swing around on a grazing one.
- The whole length of a beam melts snow, under the beam, not beside it.
- Spirit Bomb meets a beam at its surface, not its middle.

**Techniques**
- Transformations peak with a flash of light, rings and a gust of air.
- Instant Transmission no longer slides into place.
- Serpent Beam and homing shots let a target that dodged far off go instead of whipping round.
- Death Ball and Spirit Bomb no longer shake at the start of the cast, and dent harder against walls and bodies.
- Spirit Sword is slimmer and stays inside the area it hits.
- Destructo Disc casts a proper shadow in flight.
- Each lobbed technique has its own landing height: it comes down on ground up to that high above you, any distance below, and hits only the level it lands on.

**World**
- Bushes, big mushrooms and boulders are as solid as they look: walk right behind them, jump on top and stand there. The pink bush is solid too, and mushrooms and boulders stop shots that fly lower than their top.
- Creatures that turn on the spot now show it.

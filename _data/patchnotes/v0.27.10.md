## v0.27.10: Smooth Cliff Walking [Patch]

### Fixed
- Your character no longer shakes on the spot when you hold a diagonal into a corner where a cliff meets a wall or another plateau.
- Sliding along a cliff face or wall while holding a diagonal now moves at a steady speed instead of stuttering.
- Walking straight into the corner of a cliff, rock or wall that only clips the edge of your feet now slides you around it instead of stopping you dead.
- Fixed a case where you and the server could end up on different sides of a cliff corner, which pulled you back after a few seconds.

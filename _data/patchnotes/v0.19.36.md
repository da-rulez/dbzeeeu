## v0.19.36: New Threads [Patch]

Two new bodies, a rack of new clothing, and proper icons for all of it.

### New character bodies
- **Saiyan women get a second body**, with the full transformation set from Super Saiyan up through Ultra Instinct and Super Saiyan 5.
- **Namekians get a third body**, including its Super Namek and Super Shenron forms.
- Both are picked at character creation like any other body.

### New gear
- **Martial Gi.** A full jacket-and-trousers training set, recolourable in two colours.
- **Cloth Armbands** for the hands slot, recolourable, with its own fit for Saiyan women.
- **Cinch Belt**, the first item to use the accessory slot.
- **Battle-torn shirt, pants, Turtle School pants and undershirt.** Shredded versions of the everyday kit for anyone who wants to look like they walked out of the fight rather than into it.
- All of it is stocked in the admin shop.

### Icons
- Every new piece above has its own inventory icon.
- The Monkey King Gi and Potara Gi got new icons too, and the four premium furniture items now use proper icon art instead of their world sprite.

### Fixes
- Monkey King Gi and Potara Gi were pointing at art that had been renamed, so they could render as empty layers. Both now resolve correctly on every body.

### Removed
- **The Battle Damaged Goku Outfit is gone.** Its art was retired and the battle-torn pieces above replace it. If you had one equipped it will simply stop appearing.

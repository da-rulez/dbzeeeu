## v0.20.19: Spirit Bomb Glow [Hotfix]

**The Spirit Bomb's light was ten times the size of the ball it belongs to.** It now sits a quarter wider than the sphere, at every charge level: a small ball is a small glow, a fully charged one lights the field it is about to flatten.

The light was being scaled twice over, once by the ball's growth and again by a number taken from its collision box, so the glow ran away from the technique as it grew. Every other technique's light is unchanged.

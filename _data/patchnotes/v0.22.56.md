## v0.22.56 — Hit budget and piercing are separate again [Patch]

**Multi-hit techniques**
- A technique's total hit budget and how many different targets it can touch are now two independent limits instead of one overriding the other. Set both and you get that many hits spread over at most that many targets; leave one off and only the other applies.
- Previously a hit budget quietly threw the pierce number away, and a non-zero pierce silently stopped a shot from hitting the same target twice. Nothing in the game had both set, so no existing technique changes.

## v0.25.15: Exploit audit, first wave [Hotfix]

### Fixed
- **Your connection plays exactly one character, the one your account owns.** A modified client could re-send its login mid-session with any character id and act as that character. Login now binds once, to a character the account server confirms is yours.
- **No second body.** A test-world shortcut could place a second copy of a character in a test map while the first kept playing. It is now reserved for load-test servers and bot accounts.
- **Loci and matches hold what they should.** Items cannot be dropped inside a locus run or while signed up for a match; objective and clear rewards release once per character, enforced by the account server; consumables cannot be used while signed up for a match, and buffs do not follow you out of one; match signup only from the overworld.
- **Smaller holes closed:** buying a house from a player pays the seller only once the sale is confirmed; an infusion upgrades one item, never two copies; crafting checks the station at craft time; the staff tool opens for staff roles only; upkeep payments need the right rank; trades re-check distance when executed.

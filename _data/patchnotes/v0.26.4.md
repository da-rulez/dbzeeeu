## v0.26.4: Saving Restored [Hotfix]

### Fixed
- Character progress was not being saved since the 22 October release: the save system wrote a new field the live database did not have, and every save was refused. Saving works again. Anyone who stayed online kept their progress in memory and it is now written; anyone who logged out in that window may have lost progress made since 22 October. If that is you, tell us what you lost.
- Bestiary drop records, recipe discoveries and a few other one-time records had been failing silently on the live database since September. They record again.

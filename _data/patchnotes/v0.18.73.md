## v0.18.73: Anchored [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Shadows sit exactly where their setting says.** Cutting shadows loose from their spritesheet last patch left a transparent margin at the bottom, and the game was lining up the edge of that margin rather than the shadow itself, so every shadow floated slightly above where it was set.

### Under the hood
- The object editor's shadow preview no longer approximates. The lean it drew was measured as a slope where the game treats it as an angle, about a third short, and it ignored the sideways drift the sun adds. The solid shape now shows the exact overhead case, with the sunrise and sunset extremes outlined so the day's sweep is still visible, and the anchor line is always drawn right on the shadow's edge.

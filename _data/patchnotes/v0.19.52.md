## v0.19.52: True Colours [Patch]

- **The rarity colours now match the frames**: bronze, copper, silver, gold, rose, and the green-cyan of Z. They were picked before the art existed and did not line up with it.
- **Rarity is named wherever an item is described.** The detail panel puts the item's name in its tier colour and states the tier; equipment and hotbar tooltips do the same.

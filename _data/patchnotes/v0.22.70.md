## v0.22.70: Underfoot, for real this time [Hotfix]

**Fixes**
- Coins and dropped items now sort with everything else on the map: walk past one and it goes behind your feet when you are south of it and in front of you when you are north of it. The 0.22.69 notes promised this one release early; it is in the build now.
- A coin or dropped item that comes into view while it is already on its way out now blinks for you as well. Walk up to loot that has been lying there a while and you can see it is about to go, instead of watching it sit still and then vanish.
- Cleared a class of server errors around players who drop out mid-fight. Their character stays in the world for a few seconds so nobody can log out of a losing fight, and updates aimed at the connection that is already gone are no longer sent.

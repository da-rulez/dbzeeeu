## v0.25.20: Lab breakables [Patch]

### Added
- **Ten lab breakables**: small, medium and a large pair of cardboard boxes, green, yellow and metal crates in two sizes each. Shoot them open like a locker; coins fall out and the box stays open on the floor until it respawns. A second small box crumbles into a dust cloud instead of opening.
- Map editor: every breakable now has a **blocker offset**, so a tall piece blocks at its feet instead of mid-body.

### Changed
- **Lockers** use the new lab art, open door and all.
- A breakable's open art keeps its feet where the closed art stood, so a box whose lid opens upward no longer shifts.

## v0.20.41: Solum Sorts Properly [Patch]

**On Solum and Muscle Tower you draw where you stand again.** Both maps were running with the arena's depth sorting switched off, so nothing on them sorted against anything else: you drew under every NPC you walked past, under every beam that flew by, and juddered against them as you moved. The setting was there in the map file, written in a spot the engine reads as a note rather than as a setting. Both maps are fixed, and every map now gets checked for it before a release.

**Sprint after-images are back**, and on the right side of you. They had been pinned to the very bottom of the world, which put them under the floor tiles, which meant no trail at all. They now sit in the same layer as your body: over you running up, behind you running down or sideways.

## v0.36.0: Play in your browser [Release]

**DBZee in the browser**
- Play right from your browser at https://api.dbzee.eu/play/ (also on dbzee.eu under Play). No download, no install.
- Browser and launcher players share one world: same maps, same fights, same parties.
- Press **Play as guest** to start without an account. Your character stays on that browser.
- Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox on a PC. Chrome and Edge start fastest after the first visit.
- The browser keeps the game after your first visit, so the next start is much quicker.

**Starting up**
- A new loading screen with a progress bar, in both the browser and the launcher version.
- The login screen no longer cuts off its buttons.
- On slower PCs the graphics now drop to the right level in one step and remember it, instead of stuttering through several steps right after you log in.
- Preparing the graphics on first start is about a third faster in Firefox.

**Connection**
- Smoother movement of creatures and other players on a shaky connection.
- Your own shots line up better with what the server sees, at any ping.

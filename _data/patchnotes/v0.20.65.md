## v0.20.65: Put It On The Bench [Feature]

**Crafting has four slots, and you fill them yourself.** Drag items out of your inventory into the slots and the bench works out what you are making. Order does not matter, and right-clicking a slot takes the item back.

**You can see exactly which copy is going in.** The slot shows the real item, tier frame and colours included, and that specific copy is the one consumed. No more wondering which of your three gi the bench is about to take. The count under a slot is what the craft will actually take, not what the stack holds.

**Picking a recipe from the list still works** and now loads your own copies into the slots, taking the least invested one it can, so both ways in end up showing you the same thing before you commit.

**The infusion bench got the same treatment.** The piece you are working on sits in a slot of its own above the rail, and you can drag equipment straight onto it.

## v0.22.72: Heard from inside [Hotfix]

**Fixes**
- Guild chat sent from inside a Locus now reaches guildmates outside it. Messages only ever travelled one way between the two server processes, so anything you said in there went nowhere and you looked silent as well as offline.
- A player who really does leave your party while inside a Locus now frees their seat straight away, instead of sitting there as "(in a locus)" until it timed out.
- Server-wide announcements made while you are in a Locus reach you too.

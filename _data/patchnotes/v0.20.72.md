## v0.20.72: The Benches [Feature]

**Crafting is a bench you put things on.** Four slots in the middle of the window: drag items out of your bag into them and the bench works out what you are making. Order never matters. Right-click a slot to take an item back, drag another in to replace it. Pick a recipe from the list instead and it loads your own copies into the slots for you.

**You see exactly which copy is going in**, tier frame and colours and all, and that copy is the one consumed. Under each slot is what you put in over what the craft takes: 5,000/100. Drop a whole stack and it all goes in, so one drop can cover fifty crafts.

**Break Down: gear you have outgrown is raw material.** Hand a piece over with a Zeni fee and it comes apart into infusion materials. Better gear gives better materials, and strong pieces carry a chance of something from the tier above. It never fails. Infused and recoloured pieces break down too, for exactly what a plain one gives.

**Luck earns its keep.** It improves a chance by a share of itself, so 10% Luck turns a 10% chance into 11%, and it buys extra rolls on any range and keeps the best. It applies to what a break-down yields, whether a bonus lands, the value an infusion rolls, which drop table is picked, and how many of a thing that table drops.

**A table only shows its own work**, and anything you are missing ingredients for is greyed out, icon and all. A recipe you cannot afford still shows what it wants instead of going blank.

**The infusion bench matches.** The piece on the bench sits in a slot of its own, so you always know which copy you are working on. Every infusable piece you own is listed, greyed until the next tier's materials are in your bag.

The guide has a new **Luck & modifiers** page, and the crafting and infusion pages cover all of the above.

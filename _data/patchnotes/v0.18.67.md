## v0.18.67: Nothing In Front [Patch]

### Under the hood
- **Individual tiles can no longer put themselves in front of you at all.** The whole mechanism is removed rather than tuned: the per-tile sort line, the switch that went with it, and the editor controls for both. It was the thing that sent the sea in front of players a few patches ago, and the setting meant to justify it turned out to be blank almost everywhere.
- Overhangs and fringes still layer around you correctly. That is a property of a whole layer, asked for on purpose, and it is untouched.
- Both live maps were baked with the old code and the new and compared piece by piece: identical.

Nothing changes in-game with this one.

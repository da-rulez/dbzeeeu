## v0.20.55: Two Ways To Be A Namekian [Feature]

**The Namekian transformations split into two paths.** They were the same form twice over, sharing the Saiyan power curve. Now they choose:

- **Super Namek (Shenron Form)** — the attacking path. Heavy Spirit Attack and Strength, and not much else. Three tiers.
- **Super Namek (Porunga Form)** — the defensive path. Heavy defences, a big health pool, damage reduction and hard to knock around. Three tiers.

Same ki drain as before on both, so the choice is what the ki buys, not how long it lasts.

**New technique: Stone Skin.** Set your skin like rock for thirty seconds — both defences up and a cut to everything that lands. Scroll's in the shop.

**New art** for Conditioning and Ultimate Power, and all six Namekian forms.

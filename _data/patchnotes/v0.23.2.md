## v0.23.2: Portals that look like portals

**Locus pads**
- The energy on a Locus exit pad now rises from all around the pad's ring instead of standing in a straight row of bars: strands lift off the far edge behind you and the near edge in front of you, so you stand inside the column.
- Locus entry portals now show the same rising energy when you are cleared to enter. A portal that will not take you yet stays dark, and it lights the moment it would.

**Fixes**
- A client that had cached the presentation package from one server no longer refuses to start against another; it fetches the package again instead.

## v0.20.28: Primal Barrage Falls Properly [Patch]

**Primal Barrage** is thrown with both hands raised, and its meteors come down nose-first instead of sideways. Anything that falls straight down now points the way it is falling.

**Thunder Break's fist** lands with a proper explosion.

**Sound clean-up continues.** Travel loops are bounded and pull in closer, so nothing can pile into a drone in one corner of a map, and a loop whose projectile is already gone is dropped outright rather than left running.

Press the network overlay and you will find a live SFX counter on it now, which is there to catch exactly that if it happens again.

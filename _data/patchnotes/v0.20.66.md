## v0.20.66: Only What This Bench Does [Patch]

**A crafting table lists its own recipes.** Recipes can be assigned to a table now, so a salvage bench shows break-downs and a forge would show its own work, instead of every bench in the world offering the same list. A recipe assigned to nothing is still offered everywhere.

**The infusion bench greys out what you cannot pay for.** Every infusable piece you own still lists, so you can look at the next tier and see what it wants. It reads as unavailable, icon and all, until the materials are in your bag. Pieces already at max tier dim too, since there is nothing left to buy.

## v0.19.7: Standing Where They Stand [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Rock Golems no longer walk over the scenery.** Every NPC was sorting from a line 14px south of its own feet, so it drew in front of rocks it was standing behind. Crowded ground made it obvious.
- **Cast auras hug the caster again.** The silhouette an NPC's aura traces sat a sprite's-height too low, so a golem winding up looked like it was gathering the ground below itself.
- **Knockback on NPCs is predicted client-side.** A hard shove used to arrive as four position corrections eased into each other, which read as a slow drift instead of a hit.
- **Force Push no longer glows.** A projectile that declined a light was given a default one anyway.

### Balance
- **Rock Golems have three times the health.** They were folding faster than anything that heavy should.

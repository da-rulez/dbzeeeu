## v0.19.10: Point It Where You Mean [Patch]

### Fixes
- **A caster's charge aura no longer sticks on permanently.** Interrupt an NPC mid-cast, let it start another technique, and the aura it was supposed to shed became the thing it restored itself to. It could never switch off after that.
- **Line techniques aimed at a selected target now fire straight at it.** They were being snapped to the nearest of eight compass headings, so a target off-axis got a lane that visibly missed to one side. Free aim still snaps, which is what keeps an unaimed lane running straight.
- **Rock Spikes sit where they are drawn.** The spikes were rendering well below their own damage line.

### Balance
- **Rock Spikes hit a narrower lane**, matching the width of the spikes you can actually see. It was catching things well outside them.

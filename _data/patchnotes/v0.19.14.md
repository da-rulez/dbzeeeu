## v0.19.14: Reusable Art, Visible Rain [Patch]

### Tools
- **Line techniques can now nudge their hit-area art per spell.** The overlay's own offset says where the drawing sits in its frame; the new one says how this particular technique wants it sitting against its lane. One spike sheet can serve several spells at different heights instead of needing a duplicate overlay each time.
- **The weather preview shows weather again.** Its particles were being built without their sprites, which renders them as single pixels, which looks exactly like nothing at all.

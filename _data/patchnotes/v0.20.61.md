## v0.20.61: Crafting Window Polish [Patch]

**Item icons are no longer squeezed.** The recipe list, the inventory column, the ingredient rows and the output all draw icons at the size they were drawn at, tier frame and all, instead of being crushed into 24 pixels. The same item now reads the same in every window.

**The bonus has a face.** A recipe's chance at a second item shows as a proper block under the output: its icon, how many, its name in its rarity colour, and the odds in gold on the right.

**Text is on theme throughout.** Section labels are stamped headers, counts wear the mono face like every other number in the game, item names carry rarity colour, and the dividers are the same orange hairline the rest of the HUD uses.

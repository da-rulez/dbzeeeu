## v0.18.34: Mouse Keybinds, Weather & Cosmetics [Patch]

- **Bind actions to mouse buttons** — keybinds can now be set to Left / Right / Middle / Mouse 4 / Mouse 5, not just keyboard keys. Open Esc → Controls, click a bind, and press the mouse button you want. Clicking on menus and buttons still does the UI thing — your bound action only fires out in the world.
- **Frost weather fix** — the snow storm on the icy plateau now actually bites: while it's blowing, HP, Ki, and Stamina regeneration are all suppressed (unless you're protected by a passive, equipment, or an active effect). Previously it did nothing.
- **Behind the scenes:** wired up a new cosmetic transformation — **False Super Saiyan**, with a bespoke unstable crimson aura — as an Ultimate Power skin. It lands in the cosmetic shop once its sprite art is in.

See you in the arena!

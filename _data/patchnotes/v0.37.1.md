## v0.37.1: Momentum and a real portal [Patch]

**Movement**
- You now build up and carry momentum. Turning hard or reversing slows you down before you speed up the other way, and you ease into and out of a sprint.
- Running into a wall or a rock no longer builds up speed. You stop against it and pick up speed again as you slide along it.
- A jump keeps the speed you jumped with. Running out of stamina in the air, or letting go of the keys, no longer slows you down mid-jump.

**Trial Peak**
- The Calibration Locus portal is now a glowing pad east of the start. Walk onto it to go in.
- Coming back from the Locus no longer asks you to step in again right away.
- The Playtest Guide has a marker over its head, so you can see who to talk to.
- The big test world sign is gone. The intro and the guide still tell you this is a test world.

**Names**
- Account and character names can no longer pass for staff, the game itself, or the game's own bot and guest accounts.

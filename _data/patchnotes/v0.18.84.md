## v0.18.84: Five New Techniques [Patch]

### New techniques
- **Spirit Bomb.** Hold to gather it above your head. It keeps growing for as long as you charge, then sweeps down onto your own firing line and ploughs through whatever is in the way.
- **Death Ball.** The same idea, tighter and faster, and it bites harder on every pass.
- **Big Bang Attack.** A charged sphere thrown straight from the hand. Quick to build, no ceremony.
- **Human Extinction Attack.** One long wave of shards flicked up off a fingertip, raining back down across everything in front of you.
- **Hellzone Grenade.** Orbs gather around you, climb, then arc down onto your target one after another.

### Charged shots
- **Charge longer, hit more.** A fully charged Spirit Bomb or Death Ball survives several impacts instead of dying on the first thing it touches. Let go early and you get proportionally fewer, on the same curve as the damage.
- **You fire where you are pointing.** Turning while you charge now aims the shot. It used to leave along whichever way you happened to face when you first pressed.
- **The big ones cannot be knocked out of your hands.** Taking a hit mid-charge no longer breaks the pose or the technique.

### Charging up now looks like charging up
These landed quietly last patch and deserve a proper mention:
- Transformations build toward the moment they land, thickening and rising until they break at the peak.
- Hold a Ki Beam and it gathers, holds at full while the beam pours out, then settles when you let go.
- Charge while transformed and the new energy folds into the aura you are already wearing, keeping its colour.

### Fixes
- Volleys no longer silently lose projectiles on the way out.
- Big overhead techniques no longer render behind you as they leave.

Gather it, then let go.

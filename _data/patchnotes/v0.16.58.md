## v0.16.58: Rubberband Regression Hotfix [Hotfix]

Emergency fix for the constant pull-back introduced in v0.16.52. Deeply sorry for that one.

- The desync detector added in v0.16.52 measured the raw gap between your predicted position and the server's confirmed position. That gap is supposed to be large while you move (it IS the prediction), and it grows with ping, so the detector wrongly yanked every moving player back every few hundred milliseconds.
- It now measures true desync only (same-tick comparison, which carries no lag component), so normal movement is never touched: measured zero corrections above 12 pixels in normal play.
- Genuine desync recovery (wall wedges, deaths, warps) still snaps cleanly as intended.

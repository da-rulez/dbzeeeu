## v0.16.53: Generated Mountains [Patch]

First procedural terrain from the new map system.

- New mountain generator: seeded, deterministic terrain with multi-level plateaus, correctly tiled cliff faces and rims, stacked cliffs where levels meet, and walkable ramps between heights.
- Cliff rims block walking off the edge (finally!) while shots still fly over; ramps carry you between levels seamlessly.
- Internal generated test zone included; live maps unchanged.

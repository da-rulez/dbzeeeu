## v0.35.5: Fixes from the spell review [Patch]

**Techniques**
- Force Push hits what stands right in front of you again.
- Creatures aim their pushes, blasts and lanes at you properly, and their blasts are centred on them.
- Bombs land on a creature where it stands and catch it there.
- Stone Spike Lane: the spikes stand up as the hit lands, and each spike throws up dust that scatters the leaves and shakes the trees near it.
- Special Beam Cannon breaks through with one clean burst at beam height.
- A beam cut short no longer leaves invisible pieces that still hit.
- Final Flash and Spirit Bomb win their beam clashes cleanly; the clash flash is always drawn over the beams.

**Effects**
- Hit sparks show on the body that was hit.
- A charge gathered over your head no longer flashes over your hair.
- A shot's glow lies under it on cliffs and plateaus too.
- Human Extinction Attack's blasts no longer flicker or show boxes when many go off together.

**Combat feedback**
- The first hit you take after arriving somewhere plays your hurt pose and shows its damage number.
- Damage numbers show from the moment you arrive.

## v0.18.63: Above Water [Patch]

### Fixes
- **The sea no longer draws over you.** Making water solid so nobody swims off the edge of a map had a side effect: the engine treats anything you can't walk through as something you can walk *behind*, like a cliff face. So the water joined the same depth layer as fighters and painted over anyone standing north of it. Water is flat ground now, and stays under everything.

### Under the hood
- The map editor's collision overlay draws on top of trees and furniture instead of under them, so blocked cells in a wood are actually visible, and there is a new option to hide it entirely while the collision tool is up when you want to see the art underneath.

Head above water.

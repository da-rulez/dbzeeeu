## v0.25.23: Coins out of the lid [Hotfix]

### Changed
- **Chests and crates pop their coins out of the lid** in a high arc that fans out in front of the box. Lockers keep spilling theirs out of the door.
- Coins fly at the normal speed again; the shine that sweeps over a coin now pauses a random beat between passes.
- A prop opened with [E] no longer rattles like it was hit; the lid just lifts.
- Content editor: props gained a "drop arc height" field.

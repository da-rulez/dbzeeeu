## v0.19.29: Under Your Feet [Hotfix]

Corrects how the new shockwave rings sort against everything else.

### Fixes
- **Shockwave rings sit under characters again.** The previous patch put them at the caster's own depth to stop them hiding beneath the floor, which meant they could paint over anyone standing behind you. They now sort one step below the caster, the same way your shadow does, so they stay on the ground and never cover another fighter.
- **The rings are visible from the moment you start charging.** They were technically drawn before, but the charge ramp started them at a twentieth of their size and eased in so hard that they only became noticeable in the last fraction of the wind-up, by which point the effect was already fading out. They now begin substantial and widen through the whole charge.

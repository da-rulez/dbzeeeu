## v0.28.11: Beams From the Air [Patch]

- Serpent Beam has no cast time any more: jump and fire it from the air.
- A beam fired mid-air leaves from the air as one straight stream instead of zigzagging.
- The charge-up glow no longer covers your character while you face north.
- The cast bar shows for the whole cast again on 3D maps.

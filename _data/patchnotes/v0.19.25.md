## v0.19.25: Dye Works [Patch]

Picking a colour for your gear now shows the piece on your own fighter, in a palette you can actually name.

### See it on yourself before you buy it
- **The colour picker previews the garment worn on your character**, not on a floating icon. Change a shade and your fighter changes with it, equipment, hair and all.
- **Your primary and secondary colours are named** beside the preview while you pick, and the names stay on the item afterwards so you can read them off it in your bag.
- **Every swatch shows its full shade ramp** instead of a single chip, and still names itself on hover.

### A smaller, sharper palette
- **43 hand-picked colours** replace the old wall of near-identical shades, sorted into Neutrals, Blues and Teals, Purples and Pinks, Reds, Oranges, Earth Tones, Yellows and Golds, and Greens.
- **Ash Grey and Onyx Black are the undyed look.** Pick those and you get the piece exactly as it was drawn.
- **Colours now swap shade for shade.** What you pick is what lands on the cloth, with no brightness maths in between, so a deep colour stays deep and a bright one stays bright.

### Every dyeable piece repainted
- **All dyeable gear was redrawn onto the new shades**: every gi, all four armours, both capes, the shirts, pants, boots, gloves, shin guards, sashes and the scouter.

### Heads up
- **Gear you already dyed comes back undyed.** The old palette is gone, so anything you recoloured before this patch returns to its Ash Grey and Onyx Black base look rather than guessing at a colour that no longer exists. Buy the piece again to dye it in one of the new colours.

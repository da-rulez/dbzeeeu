## v0.18.45 — Beam Me Out [Patch]

Two fixes straight from player reports.

### Dimensions
- **The exit beam now appears when you clear a dimension.** On Icy Plateau — and any dimension built with the new map tools — the completion pad's beam was being drawn *underneath the floor*, so finishing every objective looked like nothing had happened. The safety exit sat elsewhere on the map and was never affected, which is why only some of the pads seemed broken.

### Combat
- **Slows wear off properly again.** A slow could outlive its own timer and keep you at reduced speed indefinitely. Your game counted the effect down but never actually removed it, relying entirely on a message from the server that could go missing if you crossed between areas at the wrong moment. It now clears itself the instant it expires. Permanent effects from gear, passives and guild auras are unaffected.

See you in the arena.

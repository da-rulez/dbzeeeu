## v0.17.1: Minigame Stability and Fair Play [Patch]

A follow-up pass on the minigames and Dragon Keys store, focused on reliability, fair play, and performance.

**Reliability**
- Matches now always resolve cleanly, even when players leave partway through. No game can hang or strand you anymore.
- Race and Dragon Ball Hunt correctly end the moment everyone else drops out.
- Tournaments handle a mid-bout disconnect instantly instead of stalling.
- Base Assault fighters now respawn at their base after they fall.

**Hosting and lobbies**
- You can now join player-hosted games straight from the Minigames menu.
- Lobbies respect their player cap, and leaving a lobby always refunds your entry fee.

**Under the hood**
- A batch of anti-cheat and server-load protections so the new systems stay smooth and fair even when the servers are busy.

Thanks for playing! More content on the way.

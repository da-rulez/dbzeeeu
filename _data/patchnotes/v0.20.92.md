## v0.20.92: Dressing Room [Patch]

**The NPC editor shows you what you are dressing.** A live preview sits under the cosmetics fields, composited exactly the way the game does it, so the colours you pick are the colours you get.

**Dressed NPCs cost less to draw.** Each one now builds only the facing it is actually showing rather than all four up front, which is a quarter of the memory for an NPC that spends its life facing south, and identical outfits are shared rather than duplicated however many NPCs wear them.

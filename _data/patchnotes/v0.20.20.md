## v0.20.20: Charged Ball Glow [Hotfix]

**Every charged-up ball now carries a light a quarter wider than the ball itself**, at whatever charge level you let it go at. Death Ball and the charged Ki Blast join the Spirit Bomb, which got this in the last patch.

Their glow used to be scaled twice over, once by the ball's growth and again by a number taken from its collision box, so a fully charged Death Ball lit a field roughly nine times wider than the sphere it belonged to. A small ball is a small glow now, and a full one lights exactly what it is about to hit.

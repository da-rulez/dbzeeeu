## v0.22.34: Session fixes [Hotfix]

- Long play sessions no longer count as "offline" or lose their login protection after 15 minutes; your session now stays alive for as long as you are actually connected.
- Server maintenance restarts of the account service no longer mark connected players as offline.
- Groundwork for the second server process: moving between processes now uses a freshly issued session token, so it works no matter how long you have been online (not yet enabled for players).

## v0.28.1: Behind the 3D World [Patch]

DBZee used to be a flat 2D game. Height was faked with draw order, so cliffs, trees and spells kept overlapping wrong. v0.28 rebuilt the world in real 3D, drawn with the same pixel art and camera angle. Every cliff, tree and character now has a real height, and the screen sorts itself by depth.

### How movement works now
Movement runs on netfox, a netcode system that rolls back and replays. Your client and the server run the exact same movement code, tick by tick. You move instantly on your own screen. The server checks every step, and if the two ever disagree it rewinds and replays from the last tick they agreed on. That means far fewer rubber bands and no more getting stuck on corners. Jumps, falls, stairs and knockback all go through the same code.

### What this patch fixes
- Shadows look like the old 2D ones again: soft, light and violet-tinted, but cast in 3D. Cliffs shade the level below them, and trees, chests and characters throw a flat stamp of their own shape that turns with the time of day.
- Shadows no longer stretch into long sheets or float loose. No more notches where stairs meet a cliff.
- Energy balls, auras, charge-ups and cast bars now sort with trees and cliffs instead of drawing over everything.
- Telegraphs lie flat over the ground, across hills and plateau edges, as one shape.
- Item icons on the ground show whole.
- Calibration Overseer: now throws a big, slow orb you can see coming, every 2.4 s.
- Minigames: no damage in lobbies or the stands, forfeits win nothing, Leave works mid-match, and the host gets the prize back if nobody wins.
- Login is safer against password guessing, and you can't delete a character while it's online.
- Many smaller fixes to boot, saves, warps and the UI.

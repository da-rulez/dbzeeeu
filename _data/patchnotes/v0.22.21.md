- Tournaments with server-side bots now actually run: every bout was skipped as a bye and bot losers were never knocked out, because the bracket code read a bot's negative id as "nobody here". Bot bouts, knockouts and advancement all work.

- Tournament bots respect the ring: their footwork stays inside the arena boundary instead of backing out of it to break line of sight.

Verified: a four-bot tournament ran semi-finals and final, all three bouts decided by knockout with real damage, zero ring exits, zero errors.

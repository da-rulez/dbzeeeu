## v0.28.19: Spirit Sword [Patch]

- New rare technique: Spirit Sword. A blade of yellow ki drawn from your fist cuts down through the cone in front of you, then back up: two separate strikes, each one a chance to hit. Learned from a Technique Scroll.
- The Collection Log now lists every technique you can learn, including the ones it used to keep secret, such as Spirit Bomb, Death Ball and the Super Saiyan forms.
- Monster-only attacks and Ultimate Power's alternate looks are no longer in the Collection Log.
- The Frost Swarm and Mountain Stomp scrolls are gone: those attacks belong to the monsters that use them.

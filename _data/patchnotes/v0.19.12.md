## v0.19.12: The Preview Actually Previews [Patch]

### Tools
- **The spell cast preview now shows the cast.** The first version drew almost nothing: it only knew how to draw spritesheets, and most cast auras are shader effects with no sheet at all. It now runs the real aura shader over a real silhouette, the real animation builder for every sheet, and the caster sprite it was pointed at a path that did not exist.
- **The preview has a self-test.** A headless check asserts the caster, the aura and the hazard tiles are actually live, so "it builds without errors" can never again be mistaken for "it works".

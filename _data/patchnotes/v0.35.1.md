## v0.35.1: New techniques and a VFX overhaul [Release]

**New techniques**
- **Final Flash** (legendary): an aggressive golden aura, a crackling ball of gold and blue electricity between the palms, then a golden beam with lightning coiling round it.
- **Big Bang Kamehameha** (legendary): a sphere with standing rings that stays between the palms while a cyan beam fires.
- **Ki Blast Barrage** (rare, held): golden bolts from left and right hand in turn, arcing out and back onto the target.

**Reworked**
- **Scattering Bullet** now fires the five bolts its description promised, in clean arcs, with the firing pose held.
- **Force Push** is a round cone of wind from the palms that knocks back and dazes.
- **Instant Transmission**: you flicker out in stripes and back in, with a puff of dust.
- **Explosive Wave**: a ki dome, stopped by walls, with a big crater.
- **Mighty Impact**: a single stomp that drives dust over the ground and leaves a crater.
- **Recovery Wave**: a new heal burst.
- Ki blasts, lobbed shards and beam charge-ups are drawn in code now.

**World effects**
- Blasts and landings push dust over the floor, sized to the blast, pouring over cliff edges.
- Shots that hit a body, wall or object flash instead of kicking up ground dust.

**Fixes**
- Charged balls (Big Bang Attack) no longer miss NPCs on your own row.
- Beams no longer leave dust puffs along their length, and the head no longer bounces on what it hits.
- Bolts leave from the palms.

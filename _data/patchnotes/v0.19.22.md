## v0.19.22: Feet On The Ground [Hotfix]

- **Every shockwave ring now expands from your feet**, not from the middle of your chest. The charge-up pulses were already anchored there; the ring on Confetti Burst, Starburst, Golden Ascension, Ember Scatter, Warp In, Prism Bloom, Gilded Arrival and Lightning Strike was not, so those read as a hoop around your waist rather than a wave leaving the floor.
- **Prism Bloom's ring** was pulled in to match, since anchoring it at the feet pushed the old size off the bottom of the effect.

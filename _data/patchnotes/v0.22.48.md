## v0.22.48: Dimensional gates and a real warp [Patch]

**Locus exit gates**
- The exit pad's glowing column is gone. An open pad now carries a proper dimensional gate: a standing oval tear that swirls into a dark centre, a bright breathing rim, sparks drawn in from around it and a light pool on the pad. It opens with a short seam-to-oval animation the moment the Locus clears, and closes again when it is sealed.

**Warping in and out of a Locus**
- Stepping through a gate now plays the Instant Transmission warp: your character strikes the pose, the warp streaks and sound play, and only then does the screen cut to black. Everyone standing near you sees the same thing.
- Arriving on the other side, in the Locus or back in the overworld, plays the warp again on your character the moment the screen comes back, so a warp reads as a warp instead of a scene switch.
- The Locus summary waits until your warp-in has finished before it opens.
- Party warps into a Locus play the wind-up on every member going in.

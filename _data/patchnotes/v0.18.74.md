## v0.18.74: Grounded [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Shadows sit under their object again.** They were floating well above it, by as much as a tile on some pieces. Objects get their anchor point moved onto the shared depth line when they spawn, and everything attached moves with them, except the shadow was quietly rewriting its own position every frame from a value that had not been adjusted. The amount depended on the object's collision, not on anything set for the shadow, so no amount of tuning the offset could have corrected it. Pillars were off by 48 pixels, the Kame House by 44.

Feet on the ground.

## v0.22.40: Locus summary redesign [Patch]

- The screen you see when leaving a locus has been rebuilt. The result header now says why the run ended and how long it took, the stats sit in a clean grid of cards, every objective shows its status as a pill with its progress underneath, and the Required and Boss badges each have their own frame at the end of the row. Long objective text wraps inside its row instead of running off it.
- Rewards are grouped per source with icons and amounts, the header and stats stay in view while the list scrolls, and the total Z Shards earned shows in the footer.

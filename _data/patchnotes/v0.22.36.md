## v0.22.36: Critical hits [Patch]

- Critical hits are in. Effects can now carry "Critical hit chance" (the share of your hits that crit) and "Critical hit damage" (extra damage on top of the base 150%). A crit shows an orange number, a gold flash and a burst on the target, plays a snap, and gives both the attacker and the victim a small screen shake.
- New setting under Graphics: Screen Shake. Turn it off to disable every camera shake.
- Also since 0.22.35: leaving a locus through its exit pad brings you back to where you came from and your party keeps your seat; players who stood still no longer look frozen when you walk up to them; an early safety exit reads "Safety exit" (dying still counts as failed); party invites point you to the Party panel (P); chat mutes that ran out clear properly, and staff can unmute from the right-click menu.

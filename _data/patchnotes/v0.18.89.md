## v0.18.89: Read The Room [Patch]

### Ability and item text now says what it does
- **Effect names stand on their own line in bold**, with everything that effect does listed underneath them, instead of the whole thing running together on one line.
- **Stat changes are colour coded.** Increases in green, decreases in red, and only the number is highlighted so "+5 Endurance" reads at a glance.
- **HP, KI and STA carry their own bar colours** wherever they appear, including inside "Max HP" and "KI Regen".
- Wording is plain now. "Cannot move" instead of "Root". "Takes 20% less knockback" instead of "KB Reduction". "25% chance to deflect attacks" instead of a bare "Deflect: 25%", which never said what the number referred to.
- Several effects used to print their own internal name at you, so buffs showed lines like "Xp Gain" and "Craft Bonus". Every effect type is described properly now.
- Fixed HP and KI rendering as "Hp" and "Ki" in every tooltip that mentioned them.

### Transformations
- **Super Saiyan 4 and Super Saiyan 5 are now Ultimate Power skins for Hair 3.**

Now you can tell what the buff actually does.

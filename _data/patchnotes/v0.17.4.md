## v0.17.4: Minigame Timing, Entry Fees, and Shop Previews [Patch]

More polish on the Minigames menu (M) and the Z-Shop.

**Clearer schedule**
- A game now shows as Coming Up a full 15 minutes ahead, then opens for 5 minutes of registration before it starts. No more games that flash "coming up" and vanish.

**Entry fees and prizes**
- Each scheduled game now has a Zeni entry fee to join. Signing up for a player-hosted tournament costs 5% of its prize pool.
- Rewards (Dragon Keys) and costs (Zeni) now sit in their own aligned columns with icons, so you can read a card at a glance.

**Host your tournament, your way**
- You can now set a custom name for a tournament you host (kept clean and within limits).

**Z-Shop live previews**
- Cosmetics now show a real preview: your character with the recolored aura, your name in its chosen color, and a frame of the death and spawn effects. What you see is what you get.
- The shop is ordered by price, cheapest at the top.

Thanks for the feedback. See you in the arena!

## v0.17.3: Scheduled Minigames and Leaderboards [Patch]

The Minigames menu (press M) got a big upgrade.

**Scheduled games**
- Minigames now run on a schedule. The menu shows what game is Coming Up with a countdown, what is Open Now to join, and what is In Progress. No more starting a game on the spot, so there is always a real crowd when one kicks off.

**Leaderboards**
- A new Leaderboards tab shows the best player in every game and every game type. Climb the ranks!

**A nicer menu**
- Cleaner card layout with each game's icon, the entry fee shown with the zeni icon, and prizes shown with the Dragon Keys icon.

Thanks for the feedback. See you in the arena!

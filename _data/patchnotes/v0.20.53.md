## v0.20.53: Dragons, Told Apart [Patch]

**Super Shenron and Super Porunga stop looking like the same transformation.** They were sharing one aura, so there was nothing to tell them apart at a glance. Shenron now burns green and Porunga burns deep crimson, on the wind-up and while you hold the form.

**New art for eight techniques:** both Namekian dragon lines at every stage, plus Sacred Water and Dragon Clan Rites.

**Rarities settled:** Super Shenron 3 and Super Porunga 3 are Legendary; the other dragon forms, Sacred Water and Dragon Clan Rites are Epic.

## v0.19.8: Hold The Note [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Knocked-back NPCs stop drifting off where the server never put them.** The shove was being re-simulated locally, and a golem shoved into a rock could settle on the wrong side of it. It now follows the server's own path with the lag taken out, so the hit still lands hard but nothing invents a position.
- **Sustained cast auras hold instead of twitching.** A spell that keeps firing sends its overlay again on every tick, and each arrival tore the aura down and swelled it from nothing again. Human Extinction shows it best: the electricity now sits at full strength for the whole stream instead of flickering back and forth.
- **Overlay animations no longer rewind mid-play.** The same repeat that reset the aura also restarted the spritesheet, so long effects only ever showed their opening frames.
- **NPCs on raised ground keep their place in the scenery.** A state packet could stamp a depth value that outranked the world's sorting, with nothing to take it back off.

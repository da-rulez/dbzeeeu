## v0.18.76: Pick A Tree [Patch]

### Under the hood
- **The copy-collision picker fits on screen again.** With seventy-odd objects in the list the window grew past the bottom of the display and took its OK button with it, so it could not be used at all. It is capped to the screen now and the list scrolls inside it.
- The rows in that picker show each object's proper name instead of its internal id, sorted alphabetically, with the id on the tooltip.

Nothing changes in-game with this one.

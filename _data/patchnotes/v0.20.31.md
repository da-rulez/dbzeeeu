## v0.20.31: Projectile Depth Rolled Back [Hotfix]

**Projectiles draw correctly again.** The last patch's attempt at sorting them against you was wrong and made it worse: shots either passed over everything or vanished behind the world the moment they left your hand. That change is reverted.

Shots are back to the depth behaviour of v0.20.28, which is not perfect but is correct: they layer against the world by the ground they are over.

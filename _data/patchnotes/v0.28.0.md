## v0.28.0: A World With Height

The whole game now runs in a world with real height, and movement has been rebuilt from the ground up.

### New
- Jump with Space (rebindable). Climb ledges, drop off cliffs, and stand on top of things.
- Knockback throws you off an edge, but never up onto one.
- Shots fly in height: a shot at a target on a ledge travels up to it, and charged balls hover overhead and fly straight when released.
- Stomp techniques hit harder the further you fall.
- Enemies follow you up and down ledges and stairs.

### Changed
- Logging back in always puts you at your latest spot in the overworld, never back inside a match, locus, house or other map.
- Dropping out of a match now forfeits at once, so the others are not left waiting.
- Your entry fee comes back if the server restarts before a match pays out.

### Fixed
- Lanes and their marks on the ground stop at real walls, and no longer stop at thin air.
- A deflected shot flies where the deflect sent it instead of turning back.
- Crafting checks room for the most you could roll.
- Furniture you pick up or cancel is no longer lost if you warp or log out at that moment.
- Dragon Balls can be seen and taken on any floor.
- Rewards that did not fit a full bag are kept and can still be claimed.
- The Z-Shop outfit list no longer shows up empty.
- Dying closes open windows and ends a dialogue.
- Hotbar cooldowns keep showing after a warp.
- Quest flags no longer get wiped when you reconnect.
- Shots now damage MOBA bases and furniture.

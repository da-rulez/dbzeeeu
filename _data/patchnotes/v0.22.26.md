## v0.22.26: Upkeep paid from the bank [Patch]

- House/base upkeep is now paid from your bank only, never your inventory, and never in part. If the bank holds enough, the full amount is taken (no login needed); if it doesn't, nothing is taken and you're warned to add Zeni to your bank before the grace period ends. Keeping upkeep out of your inventory avoids any risk to the items you're carrying.
- Guild upkeep is always paid from the guild treasury (unchanged), and still ships off until an amount is set.

## v0.22.55 — Charge-scaled piercing, and crit on every hit [Patch]

**Destructo Disc**
- Piercing now grows with the hold. A short charge punches through one target; a full one carves through five, on the same curve the damage and the size already follow.
- High critical chance, +35% on top of your own.

**Critical hits**
- Techniques can now carry their own critical chance, and it applies to every hit they land. Each hit rolls on its own, so a volley, a beam's chunks and a pierce line can each crit or not independently rather than sharing one result.

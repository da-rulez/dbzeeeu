## v0.25.16: Exploit audit, second wave [Hotfix]

### Fixed
- **Match results stick to the account that played.** A fighter who disconnected before a match resolved used to vanish from the results and the rating; a winner who dropped during the result screen lost the prize. Results, ratings and payouts now use the account recorded at signup.
- **Premium prizes need a real match.** Scheduled games pay their premium reward only when at least two different accounts fought; one player plus an alt that forfeits, or plus bot fill, pays nothing.
- **A reconnect cannot create a second copy of you.** If your body was still standing in a locus after a force-quit mid-fight, logging back in finishes that body first and loads your saved state after it, instead of spawning a second one in the overworld.
- A lobby or countdown disconnect no longer leaves a save block behind.

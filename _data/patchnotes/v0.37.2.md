## v0.37.2: Discord sign-in [Patch]

**Discord**
- You can now sign in with Discord: press **Continue with Discord** on the login screen, approve on the Discord page, and type the code the game shows.
- The code now shows large under the login box. It used to land below the bottom of the screen.
- The Discord page now tells you to go back to the game tab or window for that code.
- Every account can link its Discord from the character screen, not only guest accounts.
- Discord buttons now look like Discord: blurple with the Discord logo.
- Linked accounts see who they are linked to on the character screen, and can unlink there if the account also has a password.
- **Join our Discord** button in the corner of the login screen.

**Fixes**
- The Create account screen no longer cuts off its title and username field.

## v0.18.26: Wardrobe, transforms, HQ & cosmetics [Patch]

A roll-up of recent updates, and Linux + Mac clients are back in line with Windows.

**New gear**
- Four new outfits: Yardrat Armour, Successor's Gi, Clansman Garb, and Worldkeeper Robes (each with a female-variant look), stocked and ready to wear.

**Transforms**
- Transforms can now show a fallback look when a character has no art for a form. Super Saiyan 2 falls back to the Super Saiyan look instead of the plain base form.

**Solum Bloom HQ**
- Sergeant Aldis and the Heal Bot show up properly again.
- A corridor gate now correctly stops you before the intro sequence.
- Auto-trigger points no longer show a misleading "!" prompt.

**Store & cosmetics**
- The name-color entry shows your name in the chosen color (auto-fitting the width) instead of a raw color code.
- Titles no longer hang on "checking eligibility," and Claim only appears when you can actually claim one.
- The charge aura no longer flashes the wrong color for a frame when you start charging.
- Fixed the obsidian name color to actually be dark.

**Under the hood**
- Ongoing map-system work on how the world stores its NPC spawns, lights, and exits.

See you in the arena!

## v0.27.2: Trees and Shadows on Cliffs [Patch]

### Fixed
- Trees and other world objects on a plateau now stand on it. They draw whole, in front of the plateau's own rim and rock, instead of being cut by the cliff they grow on.
- A tree next to a stepped cliff edge is no longer sliced into strips by the steps.
- Shadows of trees and objects lie on the floor they belong to. A cliff rim no longer cuts them, a tree behind a plateau no longer throws its shadow onto the plateau's top, and a shadow never draws over a character.

### Also new since last week
- Lights only light what is behind them: a ki blast flying behind a plateau no longer makes the rock in front of it glow.
- The see-through outline works behind cliffs, cast warnings lie under rock faces, and lanes stop at the first cliff face they meet.

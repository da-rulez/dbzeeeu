## v0.18.41 — Solid Ground [Patch]

A fix-up pass on how the world is built and drawn. Cliffs behave like cliffs again, your cosmetics apply the moment you pick them, and furniture stops standing in front of you.

### Cosmetics
- **Name colours apply instantly.** Picking a new name colour used to do nothing until you changed zone. It now updates above your character straight away.
- **Titles too.** Selecting a title had the same problem, and never refreshed the name at all until you moved between areas.

### World
- **Cliffs are solid again.** You could walk into the face of a cliff and end up standing underneath it. Every cliff footprint now blocks properly from below, so stairs are once more the only way up.
- **Furniture draws in the right order.** Standing below a table put you behind it. Furniture now sorts against your character by position, the way everything else in the world does.
- **Cliff edges and corners are tidier.** One-wide ledges, spurs running off a plateau, and the seam where two different heights meet all pick the right art now instead of falling back to something approximate.

See you in the arena.

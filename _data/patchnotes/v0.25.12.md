## v0.25.12: Faster launcher downloads [Patch]

### Fixed
- **Updates download about 13 times faster.** The launcher was reading one small chunk per frame while capping itself at 30 frames a second, so a download could never go faster than about 2 MB/s whatever your connection. It now transfers on its own thread: the 36 MB game pack went from 18.5 s to 1.4 s on the same link. The launcher only ever fetches the files whose checksum changed, so a game update is the game pack alone; the larger program file is only fetched when it actually changes.

Get the new launcher from the website; a launcher you already have keeps working, it just downloads at the old speed.

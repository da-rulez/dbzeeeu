## v0.18.97: Straight And True [Patch]

### Changes
- **You can aim diagonally while standing still.** Techniques used to collapse to the four compass directions unless you were actually walking diagonally at the moment you cast. Standing still now keeps your eight-way heading.

### Fixes
- **Ground effects now line up with the lane they belong to.** The bursts of a line technique were laid out against the edge of the damaged strip instead of its centre, so they sat off to one side, and because the lane rotates with you that offset swung around as you turned. They now run straight down the middle.

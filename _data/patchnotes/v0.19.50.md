## v0.19.50: Say Why [Patch]

### Refusals tell you why
- **A technique that will not fire now says so over your character**, the same way a blocked warp does. Not enough Ki, no target near enough, silenced, still on cooldown, not learned yet, already transformed, already casting.
- **Items you cannot use say so too**, which matters when the bag is closed and you are using them off the hotbar.
- Several of these used to fail silently, which read as the key not working rather than as an answer.

### Setting
- **Battle Messages** is its own toggle in Gameplay settings, separate from Overhead Warning Popups. Turn combat refusals off and keep the rest, or the other way round. Chat still logs everything either way.

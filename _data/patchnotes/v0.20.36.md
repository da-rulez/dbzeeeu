## v0.20.36: Shots Sort Against The World [Patch]

**A fired shot now passes behind and in front of things the way it should.** Projectiles were never part of the world's drawing order at all: whatever the sorting said, a shot was drawn after everything else on screen, so it slid over trees, over rooftops, over the person you were fighting, no matter where it actually was.

They now sit in the same order as everyone else. Fire upward and the blast leaves from behind your shoulder; fire downward and it crosses in front of you; and it passes behind the scenery it is genuinely behind.

A ball held over your head is unchanged, that really is above the scenery.

Also fixes something that never made it into a patch note because it only bit inside dimensions: a projectile fired in a dimension was being spawned a million pixels outside the map. Nothing was ever hit by it.

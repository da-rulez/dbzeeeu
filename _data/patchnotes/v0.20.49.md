## v0.20.49: Mending [Feature]

**Three healing techniques, and a Namekian discipline behind them.**

- **Recovery Wave.** Hands to the sky and your own ki turned inward. Closes a wound at once, then keeps knitting for eight seconds.
- **Shared Vitality.** The same ki sent across the gap into a chosen ally. Lands harder than healing yourself does, and works on them for ten seconds after. It has nothing to give an enemy.
- **Sanctuary Bloom.** Ki spills out of you and settles over the ground as a ring of green light. Everyone standing with you is mended where they stand and keeps mending for twelve seconds.
- **Grace of Namek**, a passive. Every positive effect you place lands 35% stronger and holds 50% longer, and your own body knits quietly on its own.

**A heal only ever reaches your side.** An area heal finds you, your party, and your team in a match &mdash; and nothing else. Not the thing you are fighting, and not the player fighting you in a PvP zone, who cannot be topped up by accident or on purpose. Aiming Shared Vitality at an enemy is simply a miscast.

All four are in the Collection Log and stocked as technique scrolls.

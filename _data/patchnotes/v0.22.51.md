## v0.22.51 — Shop tabs, a warp field you stand in, and the Destructo Disc [Patch]

**Shops and the bank sort themselves**
- Both lists in a shop, and both in the bank, now carry filter tabs built from what that list actually holds. A shop selling nothing but materials never shows an Equipment tab, and the header count follows the filter.

**Warp pads**
- The standing portal is gone. A warp pad now runs blue energy up out of its own surface in separate strands, with droplets lifting off it, wide enough to walk into and stand inside rather than in front of.

**New technique: Destructo Disc**
- A charged razor of ki spun above your raised hand. Hold to build it and it grows in three visible steps, the notches around its rim cycling faster the fuller it gets, while wisps draw in across the ground and a glow opens under your feet.
- Released, it travels flat across the ground on any of the eight headings, trailing a short line of fading rings with its own shadow running underneath, and cuts through up to three targets.

## v0.35.2: Spell polish, snow and beam clashes [Patch]

**Techniques**
- **Death Ball** and **Spirit Bomb** hit more often and push for longer, with a harder shake. Death Ball leaves a crater, Spirit Bomb a wide burn.
- **Destructo Disc** now cuts through up to 4 targets and flies on.
- **Special Beam Cannon** fires twice as fast, pierces without knockback, and shows a burst on every body it goes through.
- **Serpent Beam** and every homing shot curve cleanly into the target.
- **Spirit Sword**: bigger afterimages, no ground telegraph.
- **Hellzone Grenade**: smaller orbs, gathered higher.
- **Golden Dragon Fist** leaves a bigger crater.
- **Mighty Impact**: the fist hits the ground as the dust bursts out.
- Beams clash when they meet head on, even at different heights. A pinned beam flies on when its target drops away.

**World**
- Snow builds up during a snowstorm, stops short of cliff edges with a natural border, and melts under blasts and beams.
- Leaves lie around trees, get pulled, pushed and burnt by techniques, blow away on Force Push and sink in water.
- Landing in a flower field throws up petals.
- No more scorch or snow on water. Rings and telegraphs stay on the level they land on, never over cliffs.

**Fixes**
- Screen shake works again: a rumble that builds through a wind-up, a kick on impact.
- Damage numbers no longer vanish early.
- Instant Transmission lands at the target's level and keeps facing it.
- Force Push no longer throws targets onto trees.
- Burnt thick grass re-edges properly.
- Charge effects no longer sink into raised ground.

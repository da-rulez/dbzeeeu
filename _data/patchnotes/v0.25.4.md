## v0.25.4: The view stops at the map

### Changed
- **The camera stops at the edge of the map.** Walk into a corner and the view holds at the boundary while your character keeps walking, instead of panning off into the void beside the map. It works at every zoom level, and a map smaller than your screen sits centred.

### Fixed
- **Backgrounds stay inside the map.** A parallax sky or starfield no longer spills past the edge of the map, where no tile can ever be painted over it.
- **Decoration hung on a wall sorts with that wall.** A poster or a sign on a wall is now part of it: you pass in front of it exactly as you pass in front of the wall it hangs on.

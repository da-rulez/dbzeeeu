## v0.22.4: Muscle Tower Ice, Server Stalls, Lighter Big Maps [Patch]

A bug and performance round over the parts of the game that had not been looked at closely, with every fix measured.

Muscle Tower's ice floor is back. The ice tile sheet had been accidentally cropped to its top-left quarter on August 21, so roughly 900 ice cells in that map baked to nothing and had been holes ever since. The full sheet is restored and the map bakes clean.

Big maps are much lighter on your PC. Every tree, rock and prop recomputed its day/night shadow every single frame, which on the Great Canyon (1778 objects) cost about 4 ms of every 8 ms frame at 120 fps, and 2.9 ms on the Grand Isle. Shadows now update on a staggered 1-in-8 schedule and only near the camera, for the same smooth motion at under a tenth of the cost.

Server stalls that hit everyone at once are gone or capped:
- Dropped loot used to be re-scanned against every creature and bullet nearby, every tick, per item. A big loot drop was a 100 to 240 ms freeze. It is now a 4 Hz scan over nearby players only.
- A crowd entering an area full of live projectiles, or walking across area borders together, sent hundreds of catch-up packets in one tick (up to 262 ms). Those are now paced across ticks.
- Scheduled tournaments built their whole arena the moment the signup window opened, whether or not anyone signed up. The arena is now built on the first signup.
- Every world instance announced its weather to every player on the server, not just the people in it, and lingering combat bodies of players who had left were still being sent packets for up to 20 seconds.

Also fixed: content hot-reload silently keeping stale infusion and Dragon Ball definitions, an object respawn timer error during world teardown, and a projectile sound cleanup that logged an error on every shot fired.

The server profiler now reports real CPU, splits NPC and player ticks into phases, and keeps a once-a-minute load line in production.

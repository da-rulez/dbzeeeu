## v0.18.78: The Rest Of The Greenery [Patch]

### Content
- **Six new bits of scenery are placeable**: a broad oak, a big fern bush, a low bush, and three cacti. That is every plant sprite in the set now usable, with none left sitting unused.
- The four pines that only existed as colour variants can now be placed in their original colours too.

### Fixes
- Removed the teal pine recolours. They were the same tree as the originals they were made from, near enough that you could not tell them apart in the world, so the list of trees was padded with duplicates.
- The original pines had collision left over from being measured automatically, while every one of their colour variants had since been tuned by hand. They now match their variants exactly, so a pine blocks you the same way whatever colour it is.

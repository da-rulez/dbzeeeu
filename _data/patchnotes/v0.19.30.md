## v0.19.30: Rings Everywhere [Patch]

Every charge-up now throws proper shockwave rings, not just Explosive Wave.

### Shockwave rings on all charge-ups
- **Every transformation and charged technique has visible ground rings now.** Super Saiyan through Ultra Ego, Spirit Bomb, Death Ball, Big Bang, Ki Beam, the Evil Finger, Human Extinction and the rest. The rings were always authored, they just never got big enough to see.
- **The cause was the charge swell scaling them down.** A ring already expands on its own inside every pulse, and the build-up was shrinking that ceiling on top of it, so rings stayed a few pixels wide until the moment the effect faded out. They now pulse at the size they were drawn at, while the aura, glow, sparks and lightning still swell as they always did.
- **Rings read more clearly against a lit scene**, with the faintest ones brought up so they are not lost against bright ground.

## v0.20.27: Sounds Come From Where They Happen [Patch]

**Projectile sounds are now positional.** Every shot fired, and every impact, used to arrive at full volume no matter how far away it was — so a fight on the other side of an island followed you around and piled up into an endless echo. They play at the shot now and fade with distance, one per volley rather than one per bullet, with a ceiling on how many can sound at once.

**Lobbed techniques land audibly.** A shard hitting the ground makes the sound it always should have.

**Primal Barrage falls instead of being thrown** — it is a barrage from above again, not a lob.

**Scattering Bullet** fires from the shooting pose rather than the volley one.

## v0.19.23: Feet On The Ground [Hotfix]

- **Every shockwave ring now expands from your feet**, not from the middle of your chest. The charge-up pulses were already anchored there; the rings on Confetti Burst, Starburst, Golden Ascension, Ember Scatter, Warp In, Prism Bloom, Gilded Arrival and Lightning Strike were not, so those read as a hoop around your waist rather than a wave leaving the floor.
- **Rings are a character wide again.** The biggest ones had grown into stadium markings. Everything is inside 64px now, and Starburst's three rings lie flat on the ground with the rest instead of standing up like hoops.

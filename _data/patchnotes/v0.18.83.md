## v0.18.83: Power Up [Patch]

### Charging up now looks like charging up
- **Transformations build toward the moment they land.** Start a transformation and the energy around you grows for the whole wind-up, thickening and rising until it breaks at the peak instead of simply switching on. False Super Saiyan smoulders crimson all the way up.
- **Hold a Ki Beam and it gathers.** The charge swells while you wind up, holds at full while the beam pours out, and settles when you let go.
- **Already transformed? The charge joins your own aura.** Charge a technique in a transformed state and the new energy folds into the aura you are already wearing, keeping its colour rather than replacing it. Your Super Saiyan gold stays gold, just bigger and angrier.

### Ki Beam
- Reaches roughly twice as far, and holds for about twice as long while you keep the key down.
- Fires from in front of you instead of out of your chest.

### Fixes
- Auras no longer trail a frame behind you mid-animation. During a charge-up the glow now hugs the pose you are actually in.
- The charge you see is the charge the server is running, so the peak lands exactly when the technique does, whatever your ping.
- Projectiles no longer spawn pointing the wrong way and snap straight a moment later.

Take a breath before you throw it.

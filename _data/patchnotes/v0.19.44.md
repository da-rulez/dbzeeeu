## v0.19.44: Arm Breaker [Patch]

A close-range sweep that catches everything in front of you, and a new thing to be caught by.

### Thunder Break
- **A short, brutal sweep of the arm with the current still on it.** Everything standing in the arc in front of you takes it at once.
- Hits in a 120 degree cone out to melee range, so a crowd pressing in on you is worse for them than for you. Physical damage, so it scales off Strength.
- 16 ki, six second cooldown, no wind-up.

### Electrified
- **A new condition that Thunder Break leaves behind, 30% of the time.** Ki and stamina bleed away for six seconds.
- Your stamina will not recover at all while it is on you, so it is worth breaking off rather than pushing through.

### Under the hood
- Lane techniques can now be authored as cones instead of straight lanes. Nothing existing changed shape.

## v0.16.47: Engine Groundwork [Patch]

Under-the-hood update laying the foundation for much richer maps.

- New native map system (MapKit): cliffs with real height, bridges you can walk both over and under, ramps between levels, and proper depth sorting so you can finally stand behind things.
- Projectiles and abilities are now fully height-aware on the new maps: shots pass over low walls, and attacks from a cliff top sail over ground obstacles.
- None of the live maps change yet. This patch ships the machinery plus an internal test zone; existing areas look and play exactly the same.
- Performance groundwork: the new maps render and simulate at the same speed as the old ones, with less server overhead per world.

More visible results (generated mountains, cliffs and forests) land in the next updates.

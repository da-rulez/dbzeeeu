## v0.25.17: Exploit audit, third wave [Patch]

### Fixed
- **Match results and payouts are never lost.** A result, rating or prize post that the account server did not acknowledge is now kept and retried, including after a server restart.
- **Team ratings.** In team games your rating is settled against the other team, not your own teammates.
- **Races must be run in order.** A checkpoint only counts after the one before it; jumping ahead no longer skips the track.
- **Match restore covers more.** Your transformation forms and per-item records (infusions) come back exactly as they were, like the rest of your character.
- **Locus access modes.** A guild locus requires a guild and gives each guild its own instance; in a shared locus, a party-wide objective only counts for players who were present when it was passed.

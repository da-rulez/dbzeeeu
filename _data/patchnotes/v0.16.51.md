## v0.16.51: Prediction Fix for Small Obstacles [Hotfix]

Fixes the remaining high-ping rubberbanding around small collision shapes (trees, half walls).

- Root cause found and measured: the server simulated movement in coarser steps than your client predicts, so sliding around a small obstacle could deflect each of them to a DIFFERENT side, and your position ran away until the server dragged you back. The server now integrates player movement in matched substeps.
- Measured at 150ms simulated ping grinding a tree: worst correction dropped from 74px to 16px, and runaway corrections (over 24px) from 24 occurrences to zero.
- This also smooths corner sliding everywhere, on all maps.

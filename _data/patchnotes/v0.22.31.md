## v0.22.31: Dragon Ball items restored [Patch]

- The seven Dragon Ball items are back in the game. They had been removed from the item list in an earlier cleanup, which left them with no icon or name and quietly broke several of their protections (they now correctly can't be banked, traded, dropped, or sold, on top of the safeguards added over the last day). Each ball again shows its proper star icon and name.

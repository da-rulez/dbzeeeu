## v0.18.82: Pillars Stop Casting [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Kami's Lookout pillars no longer cast a shadow.** They are drawn as part of the building, so the silhouette on the floor beside each one looked like a second object lying there rather than a shadow.
- Walls have more breathing room before they draw over you. Standing right at the base of a wall no longer tips it over your head a moment too early.

### Map editor
- Objects can now be set to cast no shadow at all, for art that already has its shading painted in or that is part of a building rather than a free standing thing. The setting is in the Objects tab and the preview honours it.

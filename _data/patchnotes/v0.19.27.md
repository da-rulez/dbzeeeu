## v0.19.27: Ground Zero [Patch]

Explosive Wave now builds before it breaks, and the spell book finally tells you what a technique costs you.

### Explosive Wave lands properly
- **The wind-up is on you now.** Ki gathers on your body through the whole cast, with shockwave rings pulsing out from under your feet and widening as the charge builds.
- **The wave itself only appears on release.** No more explosion hanging around you while you are still charging it.
- **The damage spreads outward with the blast** instead of landing everywhere at once. The front leaves you the instant the cast ends and reaches the rim on the exact frame the explosion finishes.

### The spell book reads like a spell book
- **Numbers are coloured and bolded.** KI costs carry the KI bar's colour, damage reads in orange, so you can find the number you are looking for without reading every line.
- **Effects are split by who gets them.** A technique that hurts your target and costs you something now shows two clearly separated blocks, "On Target" first and "On You" underneath, instead of one flat list you had to decode.

### Fixes
- **Cleared assets stay cleared in the content editor.** Removing an art or sound path and saving used to hand the old one straight back on reload. This affected every content tab, not just one.
- **The spell editor preview now matches the game.** Wind-up and release animations play at the right speed and time, effects sit where they actually sit, and ground rings are visible at all instead of having to be tuned blind.

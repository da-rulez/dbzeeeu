## v0.18.36 — Cures, Fixes & Stability [Patch]

A big under-the-hood cleanup pass, plus a few things that were quietly broken now actually work. Smoother fights, safer trades, and consumables that do what the tooltip says.

### Now working as intended
- **Cure & immunity consumables.** Items that grant immunity (antidotes and the like) now actually protect you — and drinking one clears the matching effect you already have. Poisoned? Down a cure and it's gone.
- **Ki-cost gear.** Equipment and effects that reduce a spell's Ki cost now truly discount your casts instead of doing nothing.

### Combat & world
- **Enemy health bars update correctly.** Monsters regenerating out of combat no longer look stuck at low HP — their bars now reflect real health.
- **Recolored items behave everywhere.** Custom-colored gear works properly in trades, the bank, and the guild vault. No more odd disappearing or mismatched stacks.
- **Smoother fights on disconnect.** Players dropping mid-battle no longer cause network hiccups for everyone nearby.

### Economy safety
- **House purchases are refund-safe.** A failed house buy always returns your currency to the right character — no more edge cases where a refund could land on the wrong player.

### Chat
- **Tighter filter.** The bad-word filter no longer falls for spaced-out or punctuation-padded evasions, while still leaving innocent words alone.

Lots more invisible hardening under the hood to keep things stable. See you in the arena.

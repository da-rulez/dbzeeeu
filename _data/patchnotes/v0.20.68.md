## v0.20.68: Room To Breathe [Patch]

**Crafting slots keep their shape.** A long count like 5,000/100 used to drag its slot wider than the ones next to it. The slot boxes are pinned and centred now, with the spare width used as padding for the number underneath, so all four read as one row.

**Infusion costs draw their materials full size.** Material icons at the bench were squeezed into 22 pixels to keep the cost line short; they draw at their authored size like every other item icon in the game.

**Counts follow the same rules as currency everywhere.** Thousands separated below 100K, then K, M and B, in the crafting slots, the output amounts and the bench's material costs alike.

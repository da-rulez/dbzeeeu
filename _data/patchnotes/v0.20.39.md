## v0.20.39: Light, Trails And Badges [Patch]

**A shot's glow no longer washes over you.** A beam passing behind a fighter painted its own light across them, which read as the beam itself cutting through the body. Projectile light falls on the world now and leaves people alone. Everything else still lights you as before, day and night included.

**Big Bang Attack is a lighter blue**, ball and wind-up both, matching the glow it gathers in.

**Sprint after-images know where they belong.** Running up they trail over you, running down or sideways they stay behind you, instead of smearing across your own sprite.

**Projectiles stop flashing at the centre of the world** for a frame as they spawn.

**Objective badges sit beside the objective**, lined up with each other, and the locus summary screen uses the same badge as the panel instead of an exclamation mark.

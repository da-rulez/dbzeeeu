## v0.35.4: Every technique reviewed [Patch]

**Techniques**
- Knockback glides about twice as long over the same distance: a push reads as a push, not a snap.
- Special Beam Cannon drills into whoever it hits for half a second, then punches through to the next one in line.
- Destructo Disc is thrown down to body height and cuts through the middle of its target. Its shadow matches the disc and no longer shakes.
- Death Ball comes down on the target you selected, wherever it stands.
- Big Bang Attack's blast is half as wide.
- Instant Transmission on a raised ledge forms right beside the target, with no turn before the warp.
- Hellzone Grenade, Finger Barrage and other lobbed shots rise from your body, not from the ground in front of you.
- Mighty Impact and other blasts around you hit a creature where it stands.

**Shots and beams**
- A shot's light falls softly on the ground under it, not as a bright patch above it.
- Beams and shots fly past tree crowns: only the trunk stops them.
- Explosions of your own big shots no longer go off short of the ball.
- Someone walking across a beam is hit where you see them.

**Creatures**
- Knocked-back creatures slide smoothly instead of jumping.

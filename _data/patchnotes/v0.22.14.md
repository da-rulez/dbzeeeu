## v0.22.14: Interact Depth [Patch]

- Interact point graphics (the Bloom chamber computer, for one) now sort by depth like everything else instead of always drawing over the player. In MapKit maps they join the shared depth space with their sort line at the graphic's bottom edge.
- Map editor: painting a collision shape replaces the collision that cell had on any layer, instead of stacking a second shape on top of it.
- Bloom HQ first chamber: more of the room is painted.

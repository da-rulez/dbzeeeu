## v0.20.32: Zeni Drops And Death Sounds [Feature]

**Enemies drop coins.** Zeni now falls from a kill the way experience does: it scatters from the body, lies there spinning, and you pick it up by walking over it. It lands in real denominations, so 45 Zeni is four gold and five silver rather than one lump, and each coin has its own weight and colour: small silver is 1, small gold is 10, big silver is 100, big gold is 1000.

The calibration bots pay out now. Most kills are worth something, and occasionally one is worth a great deal more.

**Enemies make a sound when they die**, at the body rather than in your ear, and it can be set per enemy in the editor along with their coin drop.

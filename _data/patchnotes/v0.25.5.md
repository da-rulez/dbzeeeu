## v0.25.5: The First Bloom Chamber comes alive

### Added
- **The chamber has its own light.** The room sits at its own light level now, and the screens, the body scanner and the machinery bank glow through it. One tube over the south hall has a loose connection.
- **Fog drifts through the room.** It hangs in the air and moves across everything standing in it, and it stops at the walls instead of leaking out into the dark beyond them.
- **Vents breathe.** Steam off the floor beside the machinery, coolant hissing out of the west wall of the right wing, and a heavier exhaust venting up in the south hall.

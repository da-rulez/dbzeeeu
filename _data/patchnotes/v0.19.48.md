## v0.19.48: Wear It Out [Patch]

### Battle-torn outfits in the Z-Shop
- **All four torn pieces are now premium**, 300 to 450 Dragon Keys, and three of the four take a colour of your choosing right after you buy.
- **Buy them as many times as you like.** One purchase per colour is the idea, and the shop offers you the palette each time.
- The trade for that: there is no free replacement. These do not trade, drop or sell, and if you delete one it is gone.

### Fixes
- **Equipment tooltips show their colours** instead of printing the markup at you.
- **The vital numbers hold still.** Crossing 1,000 with a larger pool no longer slides the whole readout sideways every tick.
- Thunder Break's arc reaches about half as far, matching where the sweep looks like it lands.
- The Collection Log's technique list has been cleared out and will be filled back in deliberately.

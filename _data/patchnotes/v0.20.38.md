## v0.20.38: Objective Badges, Held Balls, One Flourish [Patch]

**Objectives say what they are.** The ones you must complete to clear a place carry a required badge, and the ones that want something big dead carry a boss badge. Both explain themselves on hover.

**A ball charging in your hand goes behind you when you aim up**, and stays in front when you aim anywhere else, instead of always sitting on top of you.

**One reward flourish per item.** Claiming 67 Zeni out of a dimension played the same coin animation 67 times in a row. It plays once now, whatever the quantity.

**The glow behind a received item fits the item.** It was sized to the icon's frame rather than the picture inside it, so anything small — a coin especially — arrived in a spotlight several times its size.

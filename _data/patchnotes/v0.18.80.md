## v0.18.80: Three New Auras [Patch]

### New skins
- **Ultra Ego** joins the Z-Shop for Hair 6. Violet ki wreathes the body, jagged and flame-like, with thin purple lightning that snaps off the arms and shoulders in bursts rather than crackling constantly. 1500.
- **Super Saiyan 4 now looks different depending on who is wearing it.** Hair 6 burns a tighter, deeper crimson than the original. Hair Broly boils red with green lightning tearing out of it at random, the wildest aura in the game so far. Both 500.
- The original Super Saiyan 4 aura is unchanged on Hair 1.

### Notes
- If you already bought Super Saiyan 4 on Hair Broly, you keep it. It is the same purchase, just with the new aura on it, and you were not charged again.
- Every hairstyle is still a separate unlock, as before. Buying a look on one hairstyle does not unlock it on another.

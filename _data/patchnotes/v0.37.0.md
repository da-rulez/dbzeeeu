## v0.37.0: Playtest world [Release]

**This is a playtest build**
- New characters now go to Trial Peak after the intro. Trial Peak is a test world for trying things out, not the real game.
- A Playtest Guide stands at the start. Talk to it for potions, some gear and every technique, plus a short how-to (B opens the spell book, keys 1-7 cast).
- A portal next to the start takes you into the Calibration Locus. Leaving the Locus brings you back to Trial Peak.
- The creatures on Trial Peak leave you alone until you attack them.

**Logging in**
- You now log back in on the map you were last on (Solum Bloom HQ or Trial Peak), never inside a Locus, a match or a house.
- Logging in loads that map straight away instead of loading Solum Bloom HQ first.

**Combat and connection**
- Getting knocked back by another player no longer snaps you back a moment later.
- Other players' ki blasts now start from their hands on your screen, not halfway to you.
- Smoother movement of other players at higher pings, and less jumpy movement right after you log in.
- Your inputs reach the server a little sooner.

**Fixes**
- The afterimages behind a sprinting character now show the hair in the right place.

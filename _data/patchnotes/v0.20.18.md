## v0.20.18: Aim While You Cast [Patch]

**Techniques with a cast time no longer commit to the direction you pressed them in.** Keep holding a direction through the wind-up and the technique turns with you, right up to the moment it goes off.

- Your character turns as you aim, and everyone around you sees it turn too.
- Lane and cone techniques redraw their ground marker as you swing them, so what is drawn on the floor is still where it lands.
- Letting go of the keys is not a re-aim. The last direction you pointed is the one that fires, diagonals included.
- Ctrl and a direction now works while a cast has you rooted. It used to send nothing at all, so turning on the spot mid cast did nothing.

Bombs are unchanged: they land on your target, and re-aim between volleys.

Techniques can also be authored the old way, committed to their direction at cast start, for the ones that should be a real commitment.

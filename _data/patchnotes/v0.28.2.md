## v0.28.2: Beams, Height and Two New Techniques [Patch]

Going 3D was more than a new look. The world has real height now: you can jump onto ledges, over low things and off cliffs, and every explosion and projectile exists at a height of its own. Shots fly up to targets on a ledge, blasts reach as high as their fireball, and anything coming down from above has to be dodged sideways, because jumping only meets it sooner.

### New techniques
- **Special Beam Cannon.** Two fingers to the forehead, a crackling charge, then a drilling beam wrapped in a spiral of ki that pierces everything in its path.
- **Galick Gun.** A violet charge at the palms and a beam that tears forward with a hard shove.
- Both come as technique scrolls.

### Beams, rebuilt
- Every beam is now one continuous shape instead of a row of pieces: no gaps, smooth bends.
- Beams leave from your palms in every direction, and still last as long as you hold the key.
- The head stays where it hits and presses into the target, while the rest of the beam keeps pouring in. Let go and the beam runs into the head from behind.
- Beams that home (none do yet, see the preview) curve in smoothly, and a quick target can still slip away.

### Effects in the 3D world
- Energy balls, auras, charge-ups, cast bars and ground marks now sit in the world: trees and cliffs in front of them cover them.
- Telegraphs lie flat over hills and plateau edges as one shape.
- Fixed a small flickering icon next to anyone firing a beam.

### Fairer fights
- Big explosions now hit as high as their blast reaches, so a jump inside one no longer dodges it.
- A falling shot or bomb can't be jumped out from under, but its blast still can.

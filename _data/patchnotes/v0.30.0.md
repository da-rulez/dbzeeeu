## v0.30.0: Forms, Ki and Stamina [Release]

A balance pass built to hold however far you train, plus new looks.

**Ki and stamina**
- A technique's Ki cost now grows with the stat it scales from. The bar and the technique book show the cost you pay.
- Ki and stamina regenerate as a share of your pool: Ki 0.5 % per second, stamina 2 % per second.
- Basic techniques are cheaper. Big techniques and finishers cost a lot more.
- Some techniques now cost stamina, and a form can drain it.

**Techniques**
- Spirit Bomb, Explosive Wave and Golden Dragon Fist no longer take a share of max HP. They hit harder on power alone.
- Heals scale with your defences instead of your attack, in strength and in cost.
- Sanctuary Ward has its own icon.

**Transformations**
- Every form gives a higher flat boost plus a percentage of your own stats.
- Namekian forms match the Saiyan form of the same tier.
- Super Namek is gone. Fighters who knew it now know Super Namek (Porunga Form).
- New scrolls for the Porunga and Shenron forms.
- Namekian forms look the same on every hairstyle, and head pieces follow your skin colour.

**Ultimate Power**
- Namekians can now use Ultimate Power, with the Porunga look by default and a free Shenron look.
- New Namekian skins: Crimson Porunga, Golden Porunga, Crimson Shenron and Golden Shenron.
- New Saiyan skin: Golden Super Saiyan 4.

**Look**
- Saiyan men have new jump poses in every direction, tail included.
- A tail now sits under the hair from the side and over it from the front.

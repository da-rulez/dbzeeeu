## v0.19.0: Rock Golems [Patch]

### New Enemies
- **Rock Golems**, in plain stone and three crystal strains. Built out of the ground they stand on: heavy, slow, and almost impossible to knock around. Their armour is stone, so blunt force is a waste of a swing, but whatever holds them together answers to ki.
- They fight with thrown rock, erupting stone lanes, and the heavier ground techniques. No two strains bring quite the same set.

### New Scenery
- **Crystal rocks**, ten kinds in four sizes. The largest stop both you and what you throw, mid-sized ones only block your path, and the smallest are underfoot scatter you can walk straight over.

### Fixes
- A cliff edge variant was being sprinkled at random points down long cliff runs, where it did not belong. Cliff edges now read cleanly along their whole length.

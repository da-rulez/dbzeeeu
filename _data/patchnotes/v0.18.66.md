## v0.18.66: Flat Out [Patch]

### Under the hood
- **Nothing draws in front of you just because you cannot walk through it any more.** That link was a leftover, and it is what sent the sea in front of players two patches ago. It is gone rather than worked around: every tile that relied on it has been cut loose, so blocking a tile now says nothing at all about what draws over what.
- Cliff faces still layer around you properly, because they are drawn with a real base line rather than inferred from their collision. The two live maps are pixel for pixel unchanged.

Nothing changes in-game with this one.

## v0.19.9: Quieter [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Knockback on NPCs no longer jitters.** The correction was snapping to each server position and guessing past it in between, so a shove read as a stutter. It now eases hard instead of stepping.
- **Techniques that need a target refuse to start when it is out of reach.** No wind-up, no overlay, no Ki spent, and it says so.
- **The target box stops flashing at the middle of the map** for a frame before landing on what you selected.

### Changes
- **AoE and line telegraphs are quieter.** Thinner outline, fainter fill, softer halo. They still read as a warning without painting the floor.
- **Human Extinction's charge is less shouty at full charge.** Lightning is thinner and sparser, the glow band narrower.

## v0.35.6: Shadows, grass and a few creatures [Patch]

**World**
- Your feet sink into thick grass again, with the grass over your ankles.
- At dusk a soft shadow fades in under every player and creature, so nobody looks like they float at night. By day the sun's shadow does the job alone; the two fade into each other.

**Creatures**
- A creature that comes back after being defeated stands in its normal pose, not frozen mid-flinch and drawn too high.
- Creatures no longer walk out over the sea. The Rock Golem by the Trial Peak shore stays on the beach.

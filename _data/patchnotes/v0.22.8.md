## v0.22.8: True Colours [Patch]

- Aura light rebuilt. The shaped light showed as little squares on the ground; it is now a soft round glow in the colour of the aura's glow section, pulsing in step with it.
- Auras keep their colour at night. The darker night tint was multiplying into them, so a yellow aura read green and dark. Auras, glows, cast and buff effects now cancel the night tint and stay yellow, purple, or whatever they were painted.
- Instant Transmission no longer strips your transformation aura. Its warp flash was clearing the aura underneath when it played.
- NPCs in combat no longer jump around. The client stopped following server positions during each knockback and then snapped back when the shove ended, so every hit an enemy took ended in a jump. It now follows the server's own track throughout, and any remaining gap closes over a few frames instead of in one.
- The gray decoration stones (the ones used around houses) no longer block walking.

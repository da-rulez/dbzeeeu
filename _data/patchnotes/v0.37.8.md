## v0.37.8: Stomp and browser fixes [Hotfix]

- Meteor Stomp uses the ground pound pose, and the character no longer stays stuck in its cast pose after a stomp from the air.
- Browser: you can sign in and pick your character while the game is still preparing, instead of waiting for it first.
- Browser: fewer freezes in the first fights of a session. The loading screen now prepares the lighting the game actually uses on your machine.
- Browser: the game no longer loses messages from the server after a short freeze, which could leave shots hanging or make many appear at once.

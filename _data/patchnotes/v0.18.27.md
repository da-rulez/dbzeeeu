## v0.18.27: Charge auras in their true colors [Patch]

Your Charge Aura cosmetic now burns in the color you actually paid for.

**Charge aura colors**
- **Every aura color is now the real thing.** The charge aura used to be painted blue underneath, and every cosmetic color was tinted on top of that blue — so the Golden Charge Aura came out green, Rose came out muddy, and Shadow barely read as purple at all. The base flame is now colorless, and your chosen color is the color you see.
- **Gold is gold.** Crimson, Azure, Emerald, Violet, Rose, Frost and Shadow all now match the swatch shown in the store.
- **The default charge looks cleaner too.** With no cosmetic equipped, channeling now flares a crisp white instead of the old blue.

Nothing to do on your end — equip a Charge Aura in the store, hold your charge key, and it just looks right.

See you in the arena!

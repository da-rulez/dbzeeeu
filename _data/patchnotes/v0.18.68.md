## v0.18.68: Solid Footing [Patch]

### Under the hood
- **A cliff-climbing check was quietly not running.** The test that makes sure you cannot walk straight up a cliff face by pressing into it was asserting the opposite of what the game deliberately does, and the spot it picked to test was buried inside a plateau where nobody can walk in from anyway. So it had been failing for the wrong reason while never actually checking the thing it was named after.
- It now checks both halves properly, on all three cliff heights: walking into a cliff base leaves you at the bottom, while arriving on top of a plateau (a respawn, a warp, coming back from a dimension) puts you on the surface rather than stuck inside it. All six pass. No gameplay change, the behaviour was already correct.

Nothing changes in-game with this one.

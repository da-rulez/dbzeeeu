## v0.18.79: Hair 6 Goes Primal [Patch]

### Content
- **Super Saiyan 4 is now available for Hair 6** in the Z-Shop, alongside the styles that already had it. Same price as the others.
- That look ships with a proper fringe hanging over the face, so it reads the way it should from the front instead of the hair stopping dead at your forehead.

### Fixes
- Fringes were missing from the minigame roster and the tournament leaderboard. Those are the two places you see characters lined up next to each other, so a hairstyle that depends on its fringe looked wrong exactly where you were comparing it to everyone else. Fixed, and there is now a check that stops any future menu from forgetting it.

## v0.28.17: Golden Dragon Fist Polish [Patch]

- Golden Dragon Fist: the charge now gathers around your body instead of a ball at your hand, the dragon swings up behind you in a wide figure of eight, and its arc stays on screen.
- Area techniques are now ovals on the ground everywhere, never circles: the warning, the hit area and the burst. Lobbed techniques (Primal Barrage, Hellzone Grenade, Finger Barrage and others) and area techniques without their own shape (Mountain Stomp, Overseer Slam) hit in that oval, so they reach a little less far up and down the screen.
- Golden Dragon Fist: the dragon turns in wide curves only, its warning shows exactly the area it hits, and the hit always lands with the dragon, also on a slow PC.
- Area warnings and bursts now lie on the ground under everyone's feet instead of around their waist.
- You can no longer walk through monsters: you bump into them and slide along, as before.
- New icon for Golden Dragon Fist.

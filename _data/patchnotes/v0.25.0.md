## v0.25.0: Doors, ricochets and a screen that moves [Patch]

Transform and feel the ground go. Fire a Charged Ki Blast down a corridor and watch it come back at you.

### Combat
- **Bouncing shots.** Charged Ki Blast now ricochets off walls and off bodies up to three times, and stays in the air long enough to spend them. A technique either passes through what it hits or comes off it, never both.
- **Transformations move the screen.** Every form rumbles while it powers up, harder the higher the form goes, and heavy attacks kick the ground where they land. Human Extinction Attack finally lands like it looks.

### Doors
- **Doors.** Some open as you walk up and close behind you; others wait for your interact key and light up when you are close enough to use them. A locked one wants the right key in your bag. Shut, a door stops feet and shots exactly where its panels are.
- **Seen through a door.** The marker that shows a character standing behind solid wall now works behind a solid door too. Glass keeps the normal view.

### Settings
- **Screen shake, your way.** Escape, Graphics: one master switch, then spell casts, impacts and thunder on their own, plus a strength slider and how far away something can be and still shake you. Even at maximum it stays readable.
- **Options rebuilt.** Six tabs, and every setting is one row: name on the left, switch, dropdown or slider on the right, with an explanation on hover.

### Smaller
- Machinery and terminals around Bloom HQ hum along at a calmer pace.

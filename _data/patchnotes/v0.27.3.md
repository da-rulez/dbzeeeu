## v0.27.3: Tree Shadows [Patch]

### Changed
- Trees no longer carry a painted shadow blob under their trunks. Their shadow is the day and night one, cast by the tree's own shape.
- That shadow now starts where the trunk meets the ground, or where it meets its roots on trees with spreading roots, and leans with the sun from there. It used to slide off to the side of the tree for the whole morning.
- Characters' shadows stay pinned under their feet the same way.

### Fixed
- A tree's day and night shadow no longer includes the old painted blob as a second dark patch.

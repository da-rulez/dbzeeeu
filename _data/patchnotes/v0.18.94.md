## v0.18.94: Fissure Wave [Patch]

### New Technique
- **Fissure Wave.** Split the ground and let it run. A seam tears away from your feet and erupts along its length, each burst throwing up the one ahead of it until the line spends itself. The damage travels with the eruptions rather than landing all at once, so anything standing further down the line has a moment to move.
- A technique scroll for it is stocked in the admin shop.

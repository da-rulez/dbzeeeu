## v0.18.81: Budokai Tenkaichi [Patch]

### Content
- **Tournament Arena - Budokai Tenkaichi** is now a tournament map. Host a tournament and it sits alongside Kami's Palace and Kame Island in the map list. Fighters start facing each other across the ring, the waiting fighters line up on the sand behind it, and spectators watch from the platform.

### Map editor
- Tiles can be marked as **walls**. A wall taller than one tile now sorts from the ground it stands on rather than tile by tile, so it covers you when you are behind it and you cover it when you are in front. Before this, a tall wall's top could draw over you while you were still standing in front of its base.
- Walls standing on the same row merge into a single shape, so a long wall is one piece rather than a stack of separately sorted tiles.
- The Wall tool tints marked tiles and underlines the row each wall sorts from, so you can see what you have marked and where it will tip.

Mind the wall.

## v0.22.68: Zeni Scramble [Patch]

**New minigame: Zeni Scramble**
- Eight players, one maze, five minutes. Shoot open the sacks, chests and vaults scattered through the corridors and grab the zeni that bursts out before anyone else does. No fighting each other; the only race is to the coins.
- Everyone leaves with exactly what they collected. The scoreboard and the leaderboard are zeni grabbed, not a rating.
- Some chests spill a powerup: a sprint, faster techniques, a wider pickup reach, or a borrowed technique in your first slot. Each runs for thirty seconds and shows its timer next to your vitals.
- Boulders block a few pockets. Shoot through one and it stays open for you.
- 10,000 zeni to enter. Everyone starts on even stats with a Ki Bolt; your gear stays on for looks only.

**Breakables**
- Anything you can shoot open now flinches: a red flash and a rattle on every hit, cracks as it weakens, and a puff of dust when it goes.
- Coins and dropped items blink before they vanish, slowly at first and quick in the last second, so you know what to run for.
- Piles of coins render lighter, so a big burst no longer dips your frame rate.

**Minigames**
- "Equalize stats" now means everyone starts on the same stat levels and levels earned in the match still count. "Remove equipment buffs" now really removes all of it: speed, pickup reach and gear effects included.
- Base Assault (MOBA) starts everyone with the match loadout; gear you walked in with no longer counts, gear you find inside does.

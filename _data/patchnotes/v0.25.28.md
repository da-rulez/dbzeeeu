## v0.25.28: One Shadow [Patch]

### Changed
- **Shadows are one piece.** A character's shadow used to be drawn in layers (body, hair, gear), and it went darker wherever they overlapped. It's now a single even shadow.
- **NPC gear casts a shadow.** NPCs wearing hair, armour or a cape now cast a shadow of the whole outfit, not just the body.
- The Turtle Hermit Shell now covers the tail when you face sideways, like capes do.

### Performance
- Each character draws one shadow instead of three, which is lighter on busy screens.

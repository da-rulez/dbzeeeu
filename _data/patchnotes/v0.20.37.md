## v0.20.37: Coins, Six Deep [Patch]

**Zeni drops in six coins now.** Small copper is 1, big copper 10, small silver 100, small gold 1,000, big silver 10,000, big gold 100,000 — so a purse of any size lands as a handful you can read at a glance instead of a heap.

**You can drop your money again.** Dropping a stack larger than a million did nothing at all before, silently. Coins you throw down scatter exactly like a kill's, and they belong to whoever picks them up, so paying someone by dumping a pile at their feet works.

**Coins earned in a dimension are no longer lost.** They were being paid straight into a bag that the dimension puts back the way it found it on the way out. They join the reward pool now and come out with everything else you earned in there.

**A ball charging in your hand stops floating over the world.** It was drawing above trees and above other fighters for as long as you held it. Techniques gathered overhead — the ones genuinely held in the sky — are unchanged.

## v0.20.26: Shots Sort Around You [Patch]

**A technique you fire now passes behind or in front of you the way it should.** Shoot upward and the blast leaves from behind your shoulder; shoot downward and it crosses in front of you. Until now a shot was sorted by its middle against your feet, so it ducked behind you either way.

A ball you are still holding over your head is unchanged: those have always had their own rule and keep it.

**Editor.** The overlay preview can now hold any character pose, so an effect can be judged against the frame it actually plays over instead of against a character standing still.

## v0.19.26: Explosive Wave [Patch]

Pull every scrap of ki inward until it has nowhere left to go, then let go.

### Explosive Wave
- **A new area technique with a long fuse.** Two and a half seconds of wind-up while the ki builds and the ground starts to light up, then it goes off.
- **The blast covers the ground around you**, 220 across and 110 deep, so it catches everyone crowding you rather than everyone in the room.
- **Heavy spirit damage and a hard throw** on everything caught inside it.
- **Anything that survives has its guard torn open** for 12 seconds, with spirit defense and endurance both cut badly. Land it twice and the tear stacks.
- **You pay for it, hard.** For 20 seconds afterwards your own defenses, attack and speed drop through the floor and every technique costs more. You are detonating your own ki, and you are standing in the crater when it lands.

### Fixes
- **Frost Swarm and Mountain Stomp can be cast again.** Neither scroll technique had a ki cost recorded, and a technique that costs nothing gets cancelled the instant you start it, so both did nothing at all when you pressed the key. They now cost 22 and 45 ki.
- **The Ki Beam wind-up no longer glitches on its last frame.** It was reaching for a frame the animation never had.
- **Fixed a handful of effects that were quietly never showing up.** A few techniques had an empty slot saved where an animation should have been, so their impact and falling visuals silently did nothing.

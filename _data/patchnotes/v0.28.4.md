## v0.28.4: Serpent Beam [Patch]

### New technique
- **Serpent Beam.** A short teal serpent of ki that winds after your selected target. It flies a stretch before it strikes and only bends so fast, so a quick target can still slip it. Comes as a technique scroll.

### Fixes
- Fixed a warning the server logged at every start while it waited for the login service.

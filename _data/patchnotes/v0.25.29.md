## v0.25.29: Hair Colours [Patch]

### New
- **Hair colours.** Saiyans now pick a hair colour when they're made: Black, Mahogany, Dark Brown, Auburn, Dark Blonde, Grey, White, Lilac or Orchid. It works on every Saiyan hairstyle, male and female.
- **Beards match.** Facial hair takes the same colour as your hair.
- **Transformations keep their colour.** Super Saiyan is still gold, whatever colour you started with.

### Changed
- Saiyan hair now comes only in these nine colours. Existing characters keep the hair they have.

## v0.18.49: Tournament Day [Patch]

### Tournaments
- **Tournaments are fought at Kami's Lookout.** The arena has a sky now, with clouds drifting past while you fight.
- **Fighters waiting for their turn stand in the waiting area properly.** Everyone was being dropped on one fixed spot that no longer lined up with the arena. The waiting ground, the spectator stand and both ring corners are now read from the map itself, so fighters spread out where they are meant to.
- **There is a pause between bouts.** The bracket used to start the next fight the instant the last one ended, which made a whole tournament feel like a slideshow. The result gets a moment to land now, and the next pair is announced before they are called in.

### Maps
- **Maps can carry a proper sky.** A two colour backdrop stretched across the map, plus drifting elements like clouds that cross it while you stand still, each layer moving at its own depth.
- Every map has been re-baked, so recent authoring is live.

See you in the arena.

## v0.20.22: Transformed Ki Bar Shape [Hotfix]

**The gold outline on a transformed fighter's Ki bar now traces the bar it belongs to.** It was drawn with evenly rounded corners while every bar in the game is cut with square top-left and bottom-right corners, so the outline sat at odds with the bar underneath it the whole time you were transformed. It also picked up the crisp top edge the other bars have.

The shield portion of the HP bar had the same problem the other way round, square where the bar is rounded, and now matches too.

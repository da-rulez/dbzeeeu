## v0.19.80: Passives and New Techniques [Feature]

**Six passives**, one for every way the game can watch you fight. Slot them alongside your actives.

- **Combat Instinct** — always on. A flat edge in all four combat stats.
- **Zenkai Boost** — under a third health. Attack climbs hard while you are in danger.
- **Relentless** — three kills in ten seconds. Faster, hitting harder, ki coming back.
- **Iron Will** — four hits inside five seconds. Damage lands softer and you stop giving ground.
- **Untouchable** — six seconds untouched. Speed and spirit sharpen until something lands.
- **Last Stand** — a quarter health, held for three seconds. Heavy damage reduction and real regeneration.

They are states, not timers: a passive is live for exactly as long as its condition holds, and drops the moment it stops.

**New techniques**

- **Super Charging Fist** — throw the punch without closing the distance. Cheaper and heavier than a bolt, and it moves whoever it lands on. Half the reach.
- **Four Arm Avalanche** — five punches thrown wide that turn back in and arrive together.
- **Sunburst Volley** — one finger, one wide scatter of small suns that comes down over everything you pointed at.
- **Primal Barrage** — ki takes the shape of the beast and falls in waves.
- **Barrier is now Reflection Barrier.** Same technique, honest name: the first instant throws shots back, the rest is a guard.

**Technique scrolls for everything.** Every technique in the collection log can now be learned, passives included. Nineteen were previously listed with no way to get them at all, including every transformation. Scrolls also show their technique's own icon and rarity, so a legendary no longer looks like a starter in your bag.

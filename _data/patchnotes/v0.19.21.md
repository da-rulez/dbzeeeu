## v0.19.21: Winding Up [Patch]

Every transformation now has a charge-up of its own, and it is the loudest moment of the whole thing.

### Transformations build before they land
- **Every form has a wind-up aura now.** Not just Super Saiyan. It swells over the whole cast, holds at the peak, and releases the instant the form takes.
- **Super Saiyan 2 and 3 crackle while they charge**, harder than the finished form does. Same for 4, Broly, Vegeta, 5 and Ultra Ego. God ki and Ultra Instinct stay calm and glow instead.
- **Electric techniques got the same treatment.** Death Ball, the Evil Finger and Human Extinction Attack now spark harder during the wind-up than any form you can be standing in while you cast them.
- **Ki pulses roll out under your feet** through the whole charge, growing as it builds.

### Your aura changes colour with what you are charging
- **Charge Super Saiyan 3 while you are already Super Saiyan and the gold bleeds into the wind-up's colour, then back when it lands.** It fades both ways. Nothing snaps from one colour to the other any more, whatever you are wearing and whatever you are charging.

### Fixes
- **Toned down the god-rays** on Gilded Arrival, Golden Ascension and Starburst. They were doing far too much.

## v0.20.51: Sacred Water, Tightened [Patch]

**Sacred Water covers a much smaller patch of ground.** Half the reach it had, so standing in it is a decision rather than a formality.

**Heal flourishes play once.** The burst that lands on someone you healed used to replay for as long as the regen ticked; it plays its one pass and gets out of the way now. It also sits eight pixels lower, on the body rather than over it.

Every buff visual now follows what its effect was authored with, instead of being forced to loop. Barrier's flare plays once for the same reason.

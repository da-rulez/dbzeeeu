## v0.18.33: Movement Prediction Fixes [Patch]

A small follow-up patch.

- **Smoother movement near multi-floor areas** — fixed a client-prediction bug where your character could snag on collision from a *different* floor (furniture, walls, or props above or below you) that the server actually passes straight through, causing a rubber-band. Client and server now stay in lock-step across floors.
- **Behind the scenes:** the furniture map-placement tooling now aligns each baked piece's collision exactly to its sprite.

## v0.20.33: Zeni Drops Through Loot Tables [Patch]

**Zeni is authored like any other drop.** It sits in an enemy's loot table alongside everything else it can drop, with its own odds and its own range, instead of living in a separate set of fields. What changes is only how it arrives: coins, scattered from the body, picked up by walking over them.

The calibration bots carry a purse table now: most kills pay a handful, and one in twenty pays properly.

## v0.22.46: Interface polish [Patch]

- Buttons no longer grow when you hover or press them, so text stays crisp; they light up, dip when pressed and dim when unavailable instead. Equipment, hotbar and choice slots highlight on hover.
- Every window closes from a Close button in the bottom-right corner. Collection Log, Z-Shop and Loci used to differ.
- Character window fits without scrolling: Combat Stats header on the left, XP Orbs and Z Shards in one compact chip on the right.
- Inventory: the collapse arrow is gone and the bag list has room next to its scrollbar. Right-click an equipped item to unequip it when the bag has space. Item hover shows the name, then rarity, then the slot in brackets.
- Technique Book tooltips are the same rich style as equipment tooltips. Cooldown reduction always shows its percentage, including 100%.
- Passive and buff descriptions no longer repeat the bonuses; the tooltip lists them.
- Loci window is larger, its list highlight is no longer cut off, and the guild creation form is a proper card.
- Collection Log shows item icons at their real size.
- Titles placed before your name now show above your character in the world, not just in chat.

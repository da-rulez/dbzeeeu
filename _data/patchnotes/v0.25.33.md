## v0.25.33: Boss Bar Pacing [Hotfix]

### Changed
- **Boss bar drains at a readable pace.** The fill edge never moves faster than 1.2 layers per second, whether a hit chips the bar, breaks a layer, or the boss falls, and small hits still take at least 0.4 seconds to play. A hit that tears through more than one layer plays out within a second and a half, so the bar never trails the real value by more than that.

### Fixed
- A hit landing while skipped layers were still flashing past no longer cuts the flash short or hides a layer.
- The kill no longer snaps the last of the bar away in one frame; it drains, holds, then fades.

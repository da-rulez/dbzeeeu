## v0.19.41: Seeing Red [Patch]

A new transformation that costs you more than Ki, and a new way to lose an argument before it starts.

### Kaioken
- **A new transformation technique, open to every race.** A crimson aura, a hard jump in strength and speed, and a much shorter fuse than the legendary forms.
- **It burns Ki and stamina at once.** Ki drains fastest and usually ends the form first, while stamina bleeds the whole time you hold it. Empty either one and the technique drops on its own.
- **Your stamina stops recovering while it is up**, so sprinting in Kaioken costs you double and leaves you winded when it ends.
- Defence barely moves. The form is built to end fights, not survive them.
- No source for the technique scroll is out in the world yet. That lands with the next content drop.

### Z-Shop: Great Ape Bucket
- **A full Oozaru head, worn as a helmet.** Fangs included, 2500 Dragon Keys in the Outfits tab.
- Grants zero extra power and roughly half your peripheral vision, but nobody has ever looked at the wearer and thought "easy fight".

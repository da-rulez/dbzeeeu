## v0.16.56: Objects, Parallax and Zones [Patch]

Phase 5 of the map toolkit.

- Map objects can now live on upper levels (a tree on a plateau ignores ground-level shots) and carry render buckets.
- Parallax background layers are authorable per map and baked in; the test zone shows one behind the map edge.
- Animated tiles verified end to end from tileset definitions.
- Zone metadata is queryable by game systems (spawn areas, ambient regions, triggers).
- Off-screen world objects are now skipped by the render sorter, removing the last map-size-dependent render cost measured in Phase 2.

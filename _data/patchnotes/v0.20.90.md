## v0.20.90: Claim What Fits [Patch]

**A full bag no longer costs you the whole claim.** Claiming a locus reward used to be all or nothing per objective: room for two of three shirts meant you got none of them and had to come back with three slots free. Now you get what fits and the rest stays waiting in the Loci Panel.

**Dyeable gear asks about each piece.** Three shirts out of one run means three colour prompts, so they can be three different colours. Stackable items still ask once for the whole stack, and a big pile caps at eight prompts with the remainder offered together.

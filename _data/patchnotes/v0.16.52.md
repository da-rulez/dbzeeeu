## v0.16.52: Prediction Overhaul [Hotfix]

The definitive fix round for high-ping rubberbanding, built on a new automated netcode test rig (six movement scenarios measured at 150 and 250 ms latency).

- Fixed a client-side error that silently broke part of the new map runtime on every player's client.
- Fixed permanent wall wedges: when you and the server ended up on opposite sides of an obstacle, the old smoothing could drag you around forever; it now detects the split and resolves it instantly.
- Large genuine desyncs now resolve with one clean correction instead of a long glide.
- Server depenetration now runs in matched substeps, removing another divergence source around obstacle clusters.
- Measured: running into a wall and sliding off at 250 ms went from constant 100 to 200 px corrections to a worst case of 9 px. Tree and half-wall grinding at 150 ms are now correction-free.

## v0.20.35: Shots Know Which Floor They Are On [Hotfix]

**A projectile's floor never reached your game client.** Every shot you saw was treated as being on the ground floor, whichever level it was actually fired on. On a client that meant a seeking shot fired upstairs ignored the enemies upstairs when working out where to curve, and shots were depth-sorted against the wrong floor's layer.

The shot now carries the floor it was fired on, so what your client sees agrees with what the server is simulating.

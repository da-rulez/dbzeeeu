## v0.20.24: Projectiles Are The Size They Were Drawn [Hotfix]

**Ten techniques were being fired at the wrong size.** A projectile's art was scaled by the size of its hitbox, so anything with a box wider than eight pixels came out inflated: Force Push and the Ki Beam at four times their art, Ice Shard and the Oozaru head at two and a half, the fists and the frost shards somewhere in between. They now fly at the size they were drawn, and their glow with them. Charged shots still grow as you hold them, which is the one case where a shot really does get bigger.

**Force Push has a sound.** It was the only technique whose projectile was silent.

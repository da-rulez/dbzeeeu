## v0.19.11: See It Before You Ship It [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Rock Spikes are back where they were.** The alignment change last patch moved them the wrong way; that offset turns out to be direction-dependent and needs eyes on it, which is what the new editor tool below is for.

### Tools
- **The spell editor now previews a cast.** Wind-up, fire, the hit area and the tiled hit animation all play on the same clock the game uses, in any of the four directions, updating as you type. It reads the live form, so a number can be tried before it is saved.

## v0.20.73: A Hundred And Twenty Things Worth Picking Up [Content]

**The material catalogue is in.** 120 new materials, from Sadala Ore and Arlian Flint up to a Cut Dragon Emerald and a Locus Beacon, named for the places and peoples they come from. Ore and dust, bars and plate, cells and capsules, rough gems and cut ones, circuits, hides and reeds, machine parts, and a few things nobody has satisfactorily explained.

**They drop.** Golems come apart into ore and raw stone, and the coloured ones carry the gems they are made of. Soldiers drop wire, cloth, bolts and plating. Drones and calibration units drop what is inside them, down to the occasional scouter core. Overseers are carrying the good stuff.

**And they build.** Ore smelts into bars, rough stones cut into faceted ones, salvaged parts assemble into working modules. Twenty-four new recipes, on a forge that lists smithing work and a salvage bench that lists break-downs, each showing only its own.

**Potions are crafted now.** Sap, bloom water and reeds make the large flasks, at 120 restored. Two flasks and something rarer make the decanters, at 300. A small stamina vial fills the gap at the bottom. Mid-game work upward, so the first one you can make is usually the first one you needed.

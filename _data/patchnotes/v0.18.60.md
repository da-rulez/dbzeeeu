## v0.18.60: Layered Ground, For Real [Patch]

### Under the hood
- **Painting one ground material over another stacks it now.** Last patch made the map editor's ground able to hold layers, but you had to ask for a new one by hand first, so the ordinary act of switching material and painting still wiped out what was underneath. The layer is chosen for you: a stroke moves up only when it would have to overwrite a different material, extends a patch on the layer that patch already lives on, and erasing takes the top material off to uncover the one below.
- **Shrinking a map really removes what falls outside it.** The tiles went, but the record the autotiler rebuilds from stayed, so a cropped patch of ground reappeared the next time anything nearby was painted.

Nothing changes in-game with this one. It is groundwork for the maps coming next.

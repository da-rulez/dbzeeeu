## v0.18.98: Facing The Right Way [Patch]

### Changes
- **You now turn to face what you are casting at.** Aiming a technique at a selected target left your character still facing whichever way they last walked, so a spell would visibly leave from the wrong side of you.

### Fixes
- **Ground lanes run straight.** A lane aimed at a target sat on whatever exact angle that target happened to be at, and a heading a couple of degrees off is invisible at your feet but grows the further it runs, dropping each burst lower than the last. Lanes now snap to a true compass heading, so east is east.

## v0.32.1: Fog, Fireflies and Leaves [Release]

A second pass over the world effects from v0.32.0. All of it is cosmetic and can be turned down or off under Options, Graphics, World effects.

**Fog**
- New: low fog that drifts over the floor. It stays off raised ground, runs up to cliff faces and walls and laps up them. Leaves and dust lie under it.
- Fog belongs to a map or a weather. No map has it switched on yet, so you will not see it until one does.
- It has its own switch: Low fog.

**Fireflies**
- Fireflies are now in the world, not on top of the screen: trees and cliffs hide them.
- They gather in small bunches at trees, there are fewer of them, and they scatter when you come close.

**Leaves**
- Woods have their leaves lying under the trees from the moment you arrive. After that a leaf drops now and then.
- Leaves take the colour of the tree they fell from and are drawn as leaves, not specks.
- Charging in the woods catches the leaves around you in a whirlwind. They fly off when you stop.

**Craters and burns**
- A blast leaves a small burn on the ground.
- A crater on the rim of a cliff is one whole crater, over the edge and the top of the face.
- A burn on a cliff face is small and subtle.

**Fixes**
- Explosive Wave and other burst effects are drawn whole. The lower half was cut off at the caster's feet.

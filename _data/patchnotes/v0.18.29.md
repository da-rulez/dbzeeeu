## v0.18.29: Stability, Fairness & Anti-Cheat [Patch]

A big under-the-hood pass — dozens of fixes to stop exploits, item duplication, and crashes, plus some co-op improvements.

**Fairness & anti-cheat**
- Closed several economy exploits and item/currency duplication bugs (furniture placing/moving, trading, and more).
- Hardened the server against a range of crafted-request cheats.

**Stability**
- Fixed a bug that could wipe a character after a bad login/reconnect.
- Day/night lighting no longer pops in a moment after you log in or warp.
- Squashed error spam during zone/dimension transitions, and stopped a save being lost when the last player leaves a house or dungeon.

**Parties & co-op**
- Party loot / XP / chat sharing now keeps working after your party changes maps.
- Dimension objectives can now be marked **solo** — a teammate one-shotting things won't count toward a solo goal, so those challenges are actually yours to earn.

**Minigames**
- Tournaments: fixed self-elimination handing you the win, and timed bouts being decided by earlier-round damage instead of the current fight.
- Fixes to MOBA respawns and base handling.

**Fixes & polish**
- Buffs: unequipping an item no longer strips a same-named potion/spell buff; shields refill correctly on recast; a temporary buff can no longer be made permanent.
- Guild Dragon Ball altars are now safe when a member deletes a character.

Plus server performance optimizations throughout. See you in the arena!

## v0.16.55: Generator Suite + Map Editor [Patch]

Two toolkit milestones in one update.

- Full terrain generator suite: forests (organic tree scatter, shot-blocking thickets), plains, lakes (walk-blocking, shoot-over water) and caves, all seeded and deterministic.
- New in-editor MapKit dock: open and edit map sources directly - paint, fill and erase tiles per layer, place and move objects, draw zones, toggle layer visibility and locks, full undo and redo, gameplay overlays (collision, height, buckets), a cliff tool that reshapes terrain heights with automatic retiling, one-click generate-and-merge, and a bake button.
- All editor logic runs through an automated 28-check suite; live maps unchanged.

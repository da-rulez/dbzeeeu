## v0.19.19: Go Out In Style [Patch]

Death and spawn effects are real now. Ten of them, in the Z-Shop's Effects tab, and every one of them was rebuilt from scratch. Every aura in the game got a little heavier while we were in there.

### Death effects stay where you fell
- **Your last moment belongs to the spot you died on, not to you.** Death effects used to play on your body, which is hidden the instant you go down and gone when you respawn, so nobody ever really saw one. They now stand on their own where you fell, play out in full, and clear themselves.
- **Everyone nearby sees it**, not just for the half-second before your body disappears.
- **Your spawn effect waits for the loading screen to lift.** It was playing while the screen was still black, so the person who bought it was the one person who never saw it.

### Ten effects worth wearing
- **Confetti Burst.** A rainbow of tumbling paper thrown in every direction, over a ring of dust.
- **Golden Ascension.** The light takes you. Gold rays, then shockwaves rolling out across the ground.
- **Starburst.** A white flash and three rings punching outward through a spray of sparks.
- **Ember Scatter** and **Smoke Vanish** finish the death set. **Prism Bloom, Gilded Arrival, Lightning Strike, Petal Bloom** and **Warp In** cover your entrances.
- **Every one plays live on your character in the Z-Shop** before you spend a Dragon Key on it.

### The ground knows when you power up
- **Ki pulses roll out from under your feet.** Charging, and every transformation, now push rings across the floor. They start small and grow as a charge builds.
- Tuned per form: a slow single pulse on Super Saiyan, a hammering double on 3, heavy red waves on 4, and something almost weightless on Ultra Instinct, whose sparks now catch the light.

## v0.19.24: Winding Up [Patch]

Every transformation now builds before it lands, and the ground reacts while you charge.

### Transformations charge now
- **Every form has a wind-up aura**, not just Super Saiyan. It swells across the whole cast, holds at the peak, and releases the instant the form takes.
- **Super Saiyan 2 and 3 crackle harder while charging than the finished form does.** Same for 4, Broly, Vegeta, 5 and Ultra Ego. God ki and Ultra Instinct stay calm and glow instead.
- **Electric techniques got it too.** Death Ball, the Evil Finger and Human Extinction Attack spark harder during the wind-up than any form you can be standing in while you cast them.

### Your aura takes on the colour of what you are charging
- **Charge Super Saiyan 3 while already Super Saiyan and the gold bleeds into the wind-up's colour, then back when it lands.** It fades both ways. Nothing snaps between colours any more, whatever you are wearing and whatever you are charging.

### Ki pulses on the floor
- **Charging pushes rings out from under your feet**, growing as the charge builds, and they sit on the ground properly: anything in front of you passes over them.
- Sized to what you are winding up. A plain charge ripples; **Death Ball**, **Spirit Bomb** and **Human Extinction Attack** push waves twice as wide.
- **Spirit Bomb finally gathers.** Energy streams up off you in a thick column toward the ball above your head, instead of a few sparks twinkling at your feet.

### Fixes
- Death and spawn effect rings expanded from the middle of your chest. They come off your feet now, like the charge pulses always did.
- Rings are a character wide again instead of stadium markings, and Starburst's lie flat with the rest.
- Toned down the god-rays on Gilded Arrival, Golden Ascension and Starburst.

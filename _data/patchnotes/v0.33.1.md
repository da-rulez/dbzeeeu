## v0.33.1: Follow-up fixes [Release]

**World effects**
- NPCs leave footsteps and dust, and tall grass and snow cover their feet too.
- Footprints last minutes instead of seconds.
- Sprinting leaves no prints in tall grass, and walking prints there are fainter.
- A gust of wind rolls leaves along slowly instead of flicking them away.
- Bushes, ferns and rocks stand out of low fog.
- Standing in a crater, no grass or snow covers your feet.
- Burns and craters from area spells sit where the rings are drawn.
- Area rings are no longer drawn on top of water.

**Spells**
- Explosive Wave and Mighty Impact hit around the caster. They were centred a little below.
- A spell cast while standing on a chest marks the floor correctly.

**Performance**
- The world effects cost a little less in large fights.

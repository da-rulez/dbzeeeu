## v0.20.45: Charges Aim Where You Do [Patch]

**A charged attack no longer fires where your last one did.** The direction you steer a charge with was never cleared when the charge ended, and the release reads that before anything else — so every charge after the first left along the previous one's line until you held a direction down long enough to overwrite it. It is dropped with the charge now: steer it and it goes there, steer nothing and it follows the way you are facing.

**Sprint after-images are softer**: closer together, fainter, washed out rather than tinted blue, and stretched along the way you came so the trail reads as motion blur instead of a stack of copies.

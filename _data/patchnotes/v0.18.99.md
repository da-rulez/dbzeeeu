## v0.18.99: On The Line [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Ground bursts now erupt from the lane instead of below it.** The eruptions of a line technique were drawn a good half-tile beneath the strip they belonged to. Cast north or south that gap hid along the lane and nobody noticed, but cast east or west it sat square across it, so the effect visibly ran below the ground it was actually breaking.

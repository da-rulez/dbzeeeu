## v0.18.91: Let It Fall [Patch]

### Fixes
- **Overhead techniques no longer die the moment you fire them.** Spirit Bomb and Death Ball are held above your head, but they were colliding from the instant they left your hands, so indoors they hit the wall behind you within half a second and looked like they never appeared. They now clear walls while they are still coming down, and land properly.
- They still hit anything they pass over on the way down.
- **The charge aura no longer sticks to you.** Casting a charged technique, tapped or held, left the glow on your character permanently.
- **The Z-Shop tells you when a purchase worked.** Buying an outfit sat on "Purchasing..." forever even though you had already received it.
- Wings now have proper inventory icons.

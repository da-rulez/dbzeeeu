## v0.18.54: The Sky Is Moving [Patch]

### Kami's Lookout
- **The clouds are actually there now.** They have been spawning all along, behind the sky itself, so nobody ever saw them. They now sit in front of the backdrop where they belong, and it no longer matters what order a map's sky is put together in.
- **They drift slowly and smoothly.** They used to travel in whole-pixel jumps, which at any gentle speed read as stuttering. The motion is now sub-pixel, so a cloud can cross as slowly as you like and still glide.
- **No two move alike.** Bigger clouds cross faster, as if they are nearer, and every one gets its own speed on top of that. The sky stops sliding past like one flat sheet.
- The field is fuller: more clouds in the air at once, at a gentler pace.

### Under the hood
- Any map can carry a sky with these settings, so this is not just the Lookout. Drift speed, spread, depth and density are all per map.

Look up once in a while.

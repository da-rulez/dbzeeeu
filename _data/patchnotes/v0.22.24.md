## v0.22.24: Locus party rules [Patch]

- Dying inside a locus now removes you from the party, so you can't keep collecting the party's shared objectives from outside. (The code meant to do this was calling a method that didn't exist, so it never actually happened.)
- Leaving or being kicked from the party while inside a locus now pulls you out of the locus and ends your run there: no re-entry and no further progress from that session.
- A locus can still be run solo, including its shared "party" objectives.
- Confirmed there is no way for a party leader to disband the group and eject everyone: a party only dissolves when it drops to one member, and a leader leaving simply passes leadership to the next member.
Verified with two bots: both entered a locus together, one left the party mid-run and was ejected while the other stayed and finished solo, and a death correctly dropped the player from the party. Zero errors.

## v0.20.67: Slots Do The Talking [Patch]

**Each slot now shows what you put in over what the craft takes.** "5,000/100" under the Zeni slot, green when it covers the craft and red when it does not. The old duplicate ingredient list is gone; the slots say it better.

**Drop a whole stack and craft the lot.** Dragging a stack in stages all of it, so 5,000 Zeni against a 100 Zeni fee is fifty crafts ready to go. Max counts what is actually in the slots.

**A recipe you cannot afford still tells you what it wants.** The missing ingredient sits faint in its slot reading 0 over what it needs, instead of the middle of the window going blank, and the Craft button says Not enough rather than letting you press it for a refusal.

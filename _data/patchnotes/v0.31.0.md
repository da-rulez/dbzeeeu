## v0.31.0: Bug Sweep and Sustained Forms [Release]

A sweep through twenty parts of the game, plus changes to how forms are kept up.

**Forms and Ki**
- Ki regenerates at a flat 2 per second again, and it keeps regenerating while transformed. Regen from passives, buffs and gear counts too, so a form can be kept up with enough of it.
- Ultimate Power is the top form and now drains the most Ki.
- Charge works while transformed. It no longer restores stamina.
- Transforming and reverting no longer hands out free health: your bars keep their fill.
- Kaioken's stamina drain now works from a full bar.

**Passives**
- A bleed or poison tick counts as a hit. It breaks Untouchable and can switch on Iron Will.
- Passives with a timed effect fire again each time their condition holds.
- Dying ends hit and kill streaks.

**Combat**
- A bomb in the air still lands if you cast again, and a burst you paid for is no longer cut short.
- Bombs and shot splashes crack chests that open by being hit.
- Friendly characters can no longer be hit or taunted.
- A monster hit from far away now fights back.
- You can no longer heal across a PvP border, or heal party mates in a free-for-all.

**Items, shops and trade**
- An effect gained at the infusion bench is now applied when you wear the piece.
- Zeni orbs are no longer lost on a full bag.
- Breaking down gear uses the plain copy before a dyed one.
- Walking away from a crafting table no longer switches off loot pickup.
- Guild creation refunds the fee and token if you left before it finished.
- Bank, trade and shop refusals now say why.

**Minigames**
- Leaving or cancelling a lobby refunds the fee.
- A hosted prize is no longer lost when a bot wins.

**Other**
- Furniture can be interacted with again.
- Key doors work when someone without the key stands in front.
- Fixed several menus that could come up empty.

## v0.20.57: Kamehameha [Feature]

**The Kamehameha is in.** Cupped hands, a longer wind-up than a plain ki beam, and a heavier line of light that shoves what it hits. It costs twice as much ki per moment you hold it, so a full-length one empties you where a ki beam would not — 44 damage a chunk against the beam's 22, and a beam roughly half as long from the same pool.

Its own blue charge-up, its own beam art, in the Collection Log, and the scroll is stocked.

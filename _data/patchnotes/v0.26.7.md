## v0.26.7: Effect Popups [Patch]

### Fixed
- The lines over your character when an effect lands, is held down or comes back ("+ Name", "Name disabled", "Name restored") are one fixed 11 px size. They used to ride the damage-number animation, which grows and shrinks, so one line read bigger than the next.
- Two such lines at the same moment stack instead of landing on each other.

### Changed
- Options, Interface tab: a new "Effect popups" switch for those lines, separate from "Aura rings". Rings off hides the floor rings and the burst at your feet; popups off hides the text. The badges under your vitals stay either way.

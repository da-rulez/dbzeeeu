## v0.20.23: Techniques Get Their Voice [Patch]

**Wind-ups now sound like wind-ups.** Every technique with a charge time carries a swell sized to it: a quick tell for a half-second cast, a longer one for the two-second monsters, and the long swell for anything you hold, from a charged blast to a Spirit Bomb.

**Ki blasts crack when they leave your hand,** not when you start the cast. The shot is what makes the noise now, so it lands with the bolt and picks one of four takes at random. A spread that throws five bolts at once is still one sound, so a volley reads as a volley instead of five copies of itself.

**Punches hit like punches.** Melee techniques take a burst of three, whether the blow lands through a fist you threw or a stomp under your feet.

**Electricity crackles.** Auras and wind-ups that arc with lightning now snap at the exact moment a bolt fires, on the character it belongs to, so a Super Saiyan 2 across the field crackles over there and not in your ear.

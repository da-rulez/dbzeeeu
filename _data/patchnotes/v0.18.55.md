## v0.18.55: Kami's Lookout, Rebuilt [Patch]

### Tournaments
- **The Lookout has been rebuilt.** The arena floor is re-laid on new palace art, with fringed edges and a properly blocked rim, so you can no longer wander off the side of the world mid-bout.
- The clouds from last patch drift past it while you fight.

### Under the hood
- A pile of map editor work sits behind this one. The short version: collision now survives having art painted over it, walls can be placed on empty ground, maps can be resized, and the editor stopped hiding collision that was painted on another layer.

Fight well up there.

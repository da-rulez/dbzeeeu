## v0.22.1: Rivers Reach the Sea

Rivers no longer give up at the last low ledge. Where a wall is too small to carry a waterfall, the water now cuts straight through it and keeps flowing, down the mountainside, across the sand, and into the sea. Island streams that used to pool up on the green ring now finish the whole journey to the coast.

Behind the scenes, a big fixing and optimization round on how maps are generated, baked and loaded: building a large island dropped from minutes to seconds, several generation bugs that left maps dry are gone so every seed gets its rivers, and two big generated showcase islands joined the world data as a preview of the wilderness zones to come.

## v0.18.58: Unstuck [Hotfix]

### Fixes
- **Fixed being stuck on "transitioning" at login.** If you last logged out inside a map that has since been renamed or removed, the game tried to put you back into a place that no longer existed and left you waiting forever. You now land in the overworld instead and carry on as normal, with no lost progress.
- The same guard covers every warp, dimension and minigame transition, so a missing map can never leave you in limbo again.

Sorry about that one.

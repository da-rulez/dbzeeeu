## v0.18.75: Forty-Eight Trees [Patch]

### Content
- **48 new trees to build with.** Eight colours of broadleaf in two shapes, plus every pine and snow-pine in all eight colours: green, teal, plum, pink, amber, dark green, red and orange. All placeable in the map editor, each with collision measured off its own trunk so you can walk close behind one without catching on it.

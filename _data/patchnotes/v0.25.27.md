## v0.25.27: Regeneration [Release]

### New
- **A new way to make a character.** You wake in a regeneration vat while two scientists fill in your file. They ask where you came from, then hair, beard, tail and skin tone (only what your race has), then your name. Back on every question takes you to the previous answer.
- **Frost Demons.** A new race from **Planet Frost**, with their own look and an optional tail.
- **Tails.** Saiyans and Frost Demons can keep their tail or go without. A Saiyan's tail changes colour with their transformation, Super Saiyan through Ultra Instinct, and female Saiyans have their own tail.
- Capes cover the tail when you face sideways. Wings and the Turtle Hermit Shell leave it showing.
- New fighters start with **no orbs**. The first ones come from the people in the lab.

### Changed
- Tails, beards and hair-hiding helmets show correctly in party, guild and minigame portraits and on the character select screen.
- Party portraits no longer give bald characters a default haircut.
- Helmets that hide hair also hide it on NPCs and in minigame portraits.

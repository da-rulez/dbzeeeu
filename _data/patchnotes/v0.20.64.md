## v0.20.64: Scrap Anything [Patch]

**Infused gear can be broken down.** A piece you have poured materials into used to be invisible to its own Break Down recipe: the recipe asked for a Martial Gi, and a tier-4 Martial Gi was, as far as the table was concerned, something else. It counts now, and it yields exactly what a plain one yields. Recoloured pieces too.

**Your untouched spare goes first.** With both a plain copy and an infused one in the bag, the break-down takes the plain one, so a maxed piece is never quietly eaten while a spare sits next to it.

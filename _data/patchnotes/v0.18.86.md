## v0.18.86: Let Go [Hotfix]

### Fixes
- **Volley techniques release you the moment the last shot leaves.** Human Extinction Attack, Hellzone Grenade, Meteor Barrage, Finger Barrage and Frostbite Salvo were holding you in the throwing pose until the final projectile had already landed, up to nearly two seconds after you had stopped throwing. The animation now ends and you can walk on the last launch, while the volley is still in the air.
- Fixed those same five techniques failing to fire at all on v0.18.85.

What leaves your hand is no longer your problem.

## v0.24.1: Seen through walls

**New**
- A character behind a solid wall now shows through it. Pick the look in Escape, Graphics: Outline, Silhouette, Outline with a faint body, or Off. Attackable NPCs show red, players and friendly NPCs blue, and a second option lets you hide everyone who is not hostile.
- Only solid wall counts. Glass and other see-through tiles keep the normal view, and the marker waits until a good part of the body is hidden, so a covered fringe of hair does not light up.

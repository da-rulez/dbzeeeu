## v0.28.18: Attacks Collide [Patch]

- Shots and beams now meet in the air. Every technique that fires has a Clash Tier, shown in its tooltip (Ki Blast 1, Kamehameha 3, Spirit Bomb 5): the higher tier swallows the lower one and flies on, the same tier cancels out with a burst. Only between fighters and monsters that can hurt each other.
- Strength counts too: the stat a technique scales with, buffs included, is weighed in. At the same tier about twice the other fighter's stat wins; overpowering a technique one tier above yours takes about six times.
- A beam crossed by a stronger beam is cut where they cross: the part beyond flies off and breaks up, the rest presses into the crossing. Two equal beams head on stop each other at a clash point.
- A beam breaks where someone steps or jumps into it. Shots fired from the top of a jump fly over a beam on the ground.
- Meetings show the moment the attacks touch on your screen, also at high ping, instead of flying into each other first and bursting late.
- Monsters' shots were invisible for most of their flight and are now drawn the whole way, at the same height as beams.
- Monsters' beams look like players' beams: one glowing beam at hand height instead of a thin line at head height.
- A beam pushing someone back stays pressed against them.
- Beams light the ground along their whole length, keep their colour at night, and their fading tail still glows as it breaks up.
- Shots hit a character's body only, never the small box at their feet they walk with.

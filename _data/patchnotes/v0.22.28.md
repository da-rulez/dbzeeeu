## v0.22.28: Dragon Ball respawn hardening [Patch]

- Force-respawning a Dragon Ball now removes the old copy no matter where it is: it is taken off a pedestal, cleared off the ground, and pulled out of whoever is carrying it, before the fresh one is rolled. Previously a respawn could leave the old one in a player's bag or on an altar.
- The single-ball admin respawn now takes effect immediately on the live server instead of only after a restart.
- Respawning all balls now also clears the altars, so a pedestal never keeps showing a ball that has moved on.

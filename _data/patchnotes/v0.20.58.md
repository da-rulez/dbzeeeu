## v0.20.58: Round Numbers [Patch]

**Cast times now come in clean steps.** Every technique winds up for 0.6 seconds, 1.2, 1.8 and so on, with nothing landing between. Five were off the grid, the Kamehameha among them — it is a medium cast at 1.2s now.

**No more fractional costs.** Ki costs and damage read as whole numbers everywhere; the Ki Beam's drain per moment held rounds to 6.

Ki Charge is instant to start, as it always effectively was.

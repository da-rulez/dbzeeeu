## v0.23.1: Trees are back [Hotfix]

**Fixes**
- Trees, rocks and other placed objects were missing from every map on the new client: the presentation package listed every object but not its name, so the client could not match a map's markers to them.
- The safety exit pad in a Locus was dark and silent; it is lit again. Its "always on" flag had been treated as server-only.

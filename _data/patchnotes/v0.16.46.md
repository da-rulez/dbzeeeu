## v0.16.46: Automatic NPC Power Levels [Patch]

A quick follow-up to this morning's Power Level brackets.

**Every fighter now has a rating**
- NPC Power Levels are now derived automatically from their actual combat stats: the number shown is the amount of experience orbs a player would have to spend to match that NPC's HP, attack, defense and damage. No more unrated enemies.
- Hand-tuned ratings can still override the automatic one per NPC, so bosses can lie about their strength when the story calls for it.
- Friendly NPCs stay unrated. Who knows what the Sage is hiding.

**Under the hood**
- Bracket colors now update the moment your own Power Level changes instead of on a timer.

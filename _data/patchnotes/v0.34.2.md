## v0.34.2: Light, auras and movement [Patch]

**Movement**
- Getting knocked back no longer makes your character stutter.
- Letting go while sprinting now slows you down over a short distance instead of stopping dead.
- A jump goes as far as your run-up was fast: holding sprint from a standstill no longer gives a full sprint jump.
- You can no longer walk while reverting a transformation.

**Auras and effects**
- Auras keep their colour, by day and by night.
- Transformations and charge-ups light the ground around you in an oval and give your character a warm glow.
- Charge-ups build up smoothly instead of starting as scattered pixels.
- Auras, outlines and overlays no longer lag behind you while running.
- Explosions and shockwaves keep their colour at night.
- Aura ranges on the ground are drawn as ovals like every other area, and reach exactly as far as drawn.
- Craters are no longer cut off beside trees, furniture or small rocks.

**World**
- Lamps light their walls and floors again.

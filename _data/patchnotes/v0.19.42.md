## v0.19.42: Hat Hair [Patch]

The Great Ape Bucket now covers what a full ape head should cover.

### Headgear and hair
- **Wearing the Great Ape Bucket hides your hair and your beard.** A helmet that swallows the whole skull should not leave a fringe and a moustache poking out of it.
- **Headgear can now hide the front fringe on its own**, leaving the hair behind it visible. That is for brow-level gear that should cover a fringe hanging over the face without shaving the whole hairstyle off.
- Every place your character is drawn follows the same rules now: the world, the character select screen, and the party and guild portraits.

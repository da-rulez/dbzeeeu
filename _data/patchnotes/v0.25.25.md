## v0.25.25: Props built from furniture [Patch]

### Changed
- **Props are built from furniture pieces.** A prop names its closed piece, optional hurt stages and its open piece from the Furniture tab; their art and their collision are the prop's, so tuning a crate's footprint in one place covers the placed piece and the breakable alike. An opened prop keeps the open piece's footprint.
- Every lab locker, crate, box and chest, the MOBA and arena chests and the Zeni Scramble chest and vault now use their furniture pieces. Boulders and bags keep custom art.
- Content editor: the Props tab shows only the fields that apply (furniture pieces or custom art, hurt stages behind a checkbox) and has an animated preview of the hits, the opening and the loot scatter.

## v0.24.0: Behind the wall

**Fixes**
- Names and titles hide behind a wall together with the character. Standing behind a wall, or inside the dark space beyond a room's edge, no longer leaves a floating nameplate. NPC names and power-level tags follow the same rule.
- Wall sorting is strict: feet above a wall's bottom edge means you are behind it, one step below means in front. No more drawing over a wall while standing on its face.
- Bloom HQ, first chamber: the dark space beyond a room's edge is part of the wall and covers you when you step into it. Floor tiles no longer draw over wall art, and the tiles that went missing are back.
- Warping into the First Bloom Chamber no longer sticks on the loading screen.

**Map**
- Bloom HQ, first chamber: fresh paint on the tile atlas and half-wall collision along the corridor.

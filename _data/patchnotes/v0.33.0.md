## v0.33.0: Tall Grass, Pebbles and Power [Release]

The world reacts to you a lot more. All of it is cosmetic and can be turned down or off under Options, Graphics, World effects.

**Transformations and charging**
- Powering up pulls at the world around you: leaves come out of nearby trees, fireflies get caught in the whirl, and loose pebbles shake, drag in and rise.
- A transformation digs a crater under you while it builds, and ends in a burst that throws everything back out.
- A held charge does the same, weaker.
- You can no longer be hit or pushed while transforming.

**Tall grass and snow**
- The darker grass and flower fields are tall grass now. It covers your feet, takes footprints, and burns away where a blast scorches it.
- Snow covers your feet as well.

**Ground marks**
- Burns are darker and wider, with a ragged edge. Small hits burn only what they hit.
- Craters and burns run right up to the rock of a cliff, and a crater in grass takes its colour from the paths nearby.
- More loose pebbles on the maps.

**Seen through walls**
- A character behind a cliff shows one outline around the whole figure, hair and gear included, and its effects no longer shine through the rock.

**Options**
- New: Hide interface, for filming and screenshots.
- The world effect switches say what they cover.

**Fixes**
- Low fog follows the drawn shape of a cliff instead of its square tile.
- A gust of wind no longer shoots every leaf away at once.

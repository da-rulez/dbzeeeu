## v0.19.40: Take a Load Off [Patch]

Idle poses are here, and two of them get you off the ground entirely.

### Idle Pose tab in the Z-Shop
- **A new tab sells poses your character holds while idle.** Two to start, 1000 Dragon Keys each.
- **Levitation.** Knees drawn up, feet off the floor.
- **Meditation.** Cross-legged, eyes closed, quietly not touching the ground.
- Both hover: the character drifts up and down a few pixels and the shadow underneath tightens as you rise.
- Press **Z** to drop into your pose on the spot, or just stand still and it happens on its own. Move, cast or take a hit and you are back on your feet.
- Everyone around you sees the pose you have equipped.
- Purely cosmetic. You are exactly as easy to hit while hovering as while standing.
- The shop preview shows your own character in the pose, wearing your own gear.

### Wardrobe
- **The Battle-Torn Underarmour can be dyed** like the rest of the torn set.

### Fixes
- **Explosive Wave lost its charge aura partway through the build-up.** The shockwave rings were running a timer that tore down the whole effect when they finished. The aura now holds for the entire charge.

## v0.20.29: Shots Sort, For Real This Time [Patch]

**Projectiles finally order themselves against you and everything else.** They were never in the world's sorting space at all — every shot was drawn on top of the scene and only ever ordered against other shots. Fire upward now and the blast leaves from behind your shoulder; fire downward and it crosses in front of you, and it passes behind the things it should pass behind.

**Charged Ki Blast is gold**, and you charge it from the shooting pose. Big Bang keeps its blue.

**Super Charging Fist** is a lunge again rather than a ranged poke: it reaches about half as far.

**Four Arm Avalanche** throws from the shooting pose.

**Technique tooltips show cast time**, so a wind-up is something you can read before you commit to it rather than something you learn by dying.

## v0.16.64: Wall & Corner Prediction Fix [Patch]

Fixes the rubberband when sprinting into walls and sliding off corners at higher ping: the surge forward followed by getting pulled back is gone.

- Server corrections no longer re-apply distance the client already caught up (the cause of the forward surge and the pull-back after it).
- Corrections from server dead-reckoning gaps no longer yank the player.
- Sliding off a wall end no longer briefly freezes your movement at high ping.
- Measured on the tight-corner course at 250 ms ping: worst correction dropped from 273px to 25px, rubberband events from 26 to 2, hard snaps from 4 to 0.

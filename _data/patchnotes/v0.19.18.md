## v0.19.18: Go Out In Style [Patch]

Death and spawn effects are real now. Ten of them, in the Z-Shop's Effects tab, and every one of them was rebuilt from scratch.

### Death effects stay where you fell
- **Your last moment belongs to the spot you died on, not to you.** Death effects used to play on your body, which is hidden the instant you go down and gone entirely when you respawn, so nobody ever really saw one. They now stand on their own where you fell, play out in full, and clear themselves.
- **Everyone nearby sees it.** Not just you, and not just for the half-second before your body disappears.

### Ten effects worth wearing
- **Confetti Burst.** A rainbow of tumbling paper thrown in every direction, on a ring of dust.
- **Golden Ascension.** The light takes you. Gold rays, then shockwaves rolling out across the ground.
- **Starburst.** A white flash and three rings punching outward through a spray of sparks.
- **Ember Scatter** and **Smoke Vanish** round out the death set.
- **Prism Bloom, Gilded Arrival, Lightning Strike, Petal Bloom** and **Warp In** cover your entrances.

### And a shop you can actually judge them from
- **Every effect plays live on your own character in the Z-Shop.** Same look you get in the world, running in the preview, before you spend a Dragon Key on it.

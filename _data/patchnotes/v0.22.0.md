## v0.22.0: Fresh Waters

The world's water got a full makeover: crystal-bright rivers and lakes, brand-new waterfall art pouring over the cliffs, and hand-drawn riverbank edges wherever water meets land.

New life along the shores too: reeds sway in the shallows against the banks, red blooms grow in the deep grass, and icy rock formations dot the snowfields.

Behind the scenes we finished a full world-generation overhaul: whole mountains with winding roads to the summit, rivers that carve real basins and drop tier by tier through waterfalls, snow that slides off the peaks and gathers against the cliffs, and a guaranteed route to the top of every mountain. New wilderness zones will be built with it, and the screenshots below are straight out of the generator.

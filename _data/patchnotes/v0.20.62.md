## v0.20.62: Know It By Its Icon [Patch]

**Break Down recipes wear the piece you are scrapping.** The crafting list used to draw every recipe with whatever it produces, which meant twenty-seven salvage recipes all wearing the same lump of ore. Each one now shows the gear going in, so you find the thing you meant to break down by looking for it.

Recipes that make something still show what they make.

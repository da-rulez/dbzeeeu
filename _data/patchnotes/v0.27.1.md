## v0.27.1: Lights, Cliffs and Telegraphs [Patch]

### New
- Lights respect what is in front of them. A ki blast, an aura, an orb or a lamp only lights what is drawn behind it, so a blast flying behind a plateau no longer makes the rock in front of it glow. A plateau top is lit only by light on its own floor or above, and the rock face under a light on top of a cliff stays dark.
- The see-through outline now works for cliffs too. Walk behind a plateau and you, or anyone else back there, show up as an outline instead of vanishing.

### Changed
- Cliffs sort properly everywhere, on the generated arenas and islands too. Standing on a plateau draws you over its face, walking behind one hides you under its top, and stairs carry you from one floor to the next.
- Ground marks follow the same rules. A cast warning lies under the rock faces it reaches, and never shows on a plateau the technique cannot hit. Lanes and cones stop at the first wall or cliff face, and their mark stops with them. Bursts still spread around corners.

### Fixed
- Walking up a staircase no longer makes you blink out behind the steps for a frame.
- Logging out while not in a fight left your character standing in the world until the next server restart. It now leaves with you.
- A character who logs out mid-fight still stays in the world for a short while, as before.

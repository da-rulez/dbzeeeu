## v0.18.53: Everyone Starts At Ten [Patch]

### Minigames
- **Equalize-at-start games now put every stat at a set level for everyone, instead of dropping you to raw base stats.** Base was a weak, flat place to open a fight. Base Assault starts every fighter at level 10 in all seven stats, and you build up from there with the orbs you earn.
- The starting level is per game, so a game can open at 10, at 25, or at raw base if that is the fight it wants.
- **Your power level in these matches now reads what you are actually worth in the ring**, not the number you brought in from the overworld. So does everything in the character window: the levels, the prices, the lot.
- Everyone pays the same for the same level, as of the last patch, and now that falls out of the design rather than being a rule bolted on top.
- As always: whatever you build in a match stays in the match, and your real character is untouched when you walk out.

Ten and climbing.

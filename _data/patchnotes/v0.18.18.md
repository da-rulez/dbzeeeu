## v0.18.18: Cliffs, Water, and Sprint Fixes [Patch]

- Fixed sprinting: if you ran out of stamina while holding Shift, you now sprint again the instant stamina recovers, instead of being stuck walking until you release Shift.
- Cliff faces now always render behind you when you stand in front of them at the lower level (the bottom row no longer draws over you).
- Stairs: you can no longer slip sideways off the middle of a staircase into the cliff wall.
- Generated water (rivers and lakes) now blocks movement at its banks, so you can't walk into the water.

See you in the arena!

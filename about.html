About the game

This is DBZee.

DBZee is a free online action ORPG inspired by Dragon Ball Z. You roll a character, you pick a race, you learn techniques, you fight, you transform, you join a guild, and you chase the seven Dragon Balls across a persistent multiplayer world.

You walk into a cave transformed and watch the gold light from your aura pool around your feet. You clear a Nightmare dimension at 03:00 in a thunderstorm and walk out with a chair nobody else has. You spend months hunting one ball, then your guild loses it to a thief in ten seconds.

What's in the box

  • Deep character customization. Multiple races, each with their own stat curves and transformation paths. A big tree of techniques and passive skills. Visible hairs, gi, hats, accessories and equipment that all show on your character and reshape your aura in transformation.
  • Real time action combat. Cast bars, channels, interrupts, snipes, melee strings, and an honest PvP loot grace. Same combat system whether you're soloing in the overworld or in a guild war.
  • Epic boss fights, solo or with friends. Multi phase encounters that scale to your group. One player is a brutal puzzle. Five is a coordinated dance. Boss only drops you can't get anywhere else.
  • Dimensions to clear. Each one has bonus objectives layered on top: no hit clears, speed runs, hidden chests. Beating them drops unique furniture, paperdolls, technique unlocks, and keys to new areas that weren't even on your map.
  • An overworld worth poking at. Portals to new dimensions, hidden triggers, breakable walls, and secrets your guild won't share with the next guild.
  • Guild wars and the seven Dragon Balls. Your guild owns a base with pedestals. Hold all seven balls on those pedestals for one continuous second and your guild gets a wish.
  • Player housing and crafting. Own a house, fill it with furniture you earned, layer it however you want. Craft food buffs, gear, and decor at your own benches. Other players can visit.
  • A handcrafted pixel art world. Built scene by scene, then brought to life by dynamic lighting and weather so the same place at noon doesn't feel like the same place at 3 AM in a storm. The visual style is the game's signature, and it's how every fight, every transformation, and every dimension reads.

Who this is for

If you grew up on Saturday morning episodes and you've ever wanted to actually live in that world with other people for a while, and not just watch a fight scene, that's the audience. DBZee is a love letter wearing a netcode hat.

Status

We're in open development. The latest milestone added the transformation system with dynamic lighting (see the news page). You can grab a build today and play; expect rough edges, expect us to ship fixes weekly.

About the game

This is DBZee.

DBZee is a free online action ORPG built by a small team of obsessives. It pulls its silhouette from the source material we all grew up with — the auras, the transformations, the seven balls scattered across a world — and rebuilds the loop as a real persistent multiplayer game.

You make a character. You train. You take hits. You learn the rotation. You walk into a cave transformed and watch the gold light from your aura pool around your feet. You join a guild. You spend months chasing the last ball you need.

What's in the box

  • A persistent online world stitched from many scenes, with cross-scene chat, party, and guild systems.
  • Real-time combat built around targeted casts, channels, interrupts, and an honest PvP loot grace period.
  • Transformations driven by shaders that wrap your actual silhouette — switch hats mid-form and the aura ring reshapes.
  • Dynamic 2D lighting — auras emit Light2D, day/night cycle composites cleanly with per-scene tint, caves get genuinely dark.
  • Seven Dragon Balls, one of each, single-instance across the whole world. Wish points are awarded when a guild holds all seven on its pedestals for one continuous second.
  • Guild wars with placement and theft mechanics: 10-second casts, interruptions on movement or damage, and global chat broadcasts on every pickup.
  • An admin layer for ops with a full activity log, manual respawns, and per-ball lookups.

How we built it

The client is a single Godot 4 binary — no launcher, no installer chrome. The server is a separate Godot headless process talking to a dedicated auth/persistence service. State is durable: every Dragon Ball, every pedestal, every ground-loot grace timer survives a server restart and resumes where it left off.

Networking is hand-tuned, not bolted on. We treat netcode as a first-class feature — interest sets, late-join sync, deterministic spawn ordering, the works. If you've felt other indie ORPGs go rubber-band on you, that's the thing we set out to not be.

Who this is for

If you grew up on Saturday morning episodes and you've ever wanted to actually live in that world with other people for a while — and not just watch a fight scene — that's the audience. DBZee is a love letter wearing a netcode hat.

Status

We're in open development. The latest milestone added the transformation system with dynamic lighting (see the news page). You can grab a build today and play; expect rough edges, expect us to ship fixes weekly.

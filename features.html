Features

What's actually in the game.

Eight pillars. Eight ways to spend your time. Pick whichever one you showed up for. The others will be waiting.

Customization & growth

Make a fighter that's actually yours

From the first character creation screen to the final transformation, DBZee is built around letting you carve out exactly the silhouette, stat line, and feel you want.

  • Race & character selection. Pick your race at creation — Saiyan, Namekian, and more. Each has its own bodies, hair sets, stat curves, and transformation paths.
  • Techniques. Active abilities you bind to your bar. Long channels, instant snaps, point blank bursts. Each has a feel.
  • Passive skills. A wide tree of always on modifiers that change how your kit plays, not just its damage numbers.
  • Paperdolls & dyes. Visible gi, armour, capes, hair. Recolour gear from a full palette. Swap mid fight and your transformation aura reshapes around the new silhouette.
  • Transformations. SSJ, SSG, MUI, and more. Shader driven auras that reshape to your silhouette, each with their own buffs and drawbacks.
Action combat

Real time. Cast bars. Interrupts. Buffs you can feel.

Every encounter runs the same server-authoritative combat system, whether you're picking off a wandering NPC, clearing a dimension, or fighting over a Dragon Ball.

  • Spells & techniques. Projectiles, snipes, area bursts, oriented hazard lanes. Each with cast bars you can interrupt and be interrupted on.
  • Status effects & shields. Stacking buffs and debuffs, damage-over-time, shields, roots, silences — with immunities and tenacity that actually matter.
  • NPC AI. Enemies pull threat, kite, keep their distance or close in, and retreat when injured. No standing still target dummies.
  • Passive skills in combat. Your tree shapes the rotation, not just the post fight math. Kill-streak triggers, regen on hit, cast speed under threshold.
  • Honest PvP. Server-authoritative movement and damage, damage-based loot credit, immediate saves on exploitable events. The netcode is treated as a first class feature.
Collection log

Log every kill. Uncover every drop.

A running record of everything you've fought and found — for the players who want to see the whole game, not just clear it.

  • Enemy entries. Every NPC you kill fills out its page. Kill tiers progressively unlock its lore, stats, and full drop table the more you fight it.
  • Drop tracking. See exactly what each enemy can drop and tick off what you've already pulled.
  • Milestones. Hit kill and collection milestones for rewards and titles that show off the grind.
  • Hidden entries. Some enemies and rare drops stay ??? until you personally find them — no wiki spoilers baked in.
Minigames & tournaments

Drop into the arena

Structured PvP modes with lobbies, rounds, and payouts — a break from the open world where the only thing that matters is who's left standing.

  • Free-for-all & team modes. Deathmatch, King of the Hill, MOBA style lanes, and a Dragon Ball hunt. Solo or squad up.
  • Races. Time-trial courses run against the rest of the lobby.
  • Tournaments. Full single-elimination brackets with seeding, byes, and prize payouts — last fighter standing takes the pot.
  • Rewards & records. Zeni and premium-currency payouts, per-mode win tracking, and titles for the players who keep showing up on top.
Hone your skills

Dimensions to clear, objectives to chase

Portals lead to self-contained challenge zones with their own tilesets, NPCs, and rules. Clear them, then clear them harder for the real rewards.

  • Clearable dimensions. Each has a primary objective (usually beat the boss) with escalating difficulty.
  • Bonus objectives. No-hit clears, speed clears, full clears, find the hidden chest. Stackable. Optional. Brutal at the high end.
  • AND / OR reward trees. Some rewards you all get; some make you choose. Items, spells, XP, currency, cosmetic auras, and paperdoll skins.
  • New areas to find. Some unlocks aren't on a vendor — they drop from a clear nobody's done yet.
Dynamic weather

Storms roll in. Daylight burns out.

Weather and time of day are real, server-driven systems, not background images. The same zone feels different at 3 AM in a thunderstorm than at noon in clear sun.

  • Per scene weather. Each zone has its own weighted pool: rain, snow, fog, lightning, dust. They roll in, build, and pass on their own clocks.
  • Day / night cycle. A global day length feeds every outdoor scene, tinting the whole world from dawn through night.
  • It reads on your kit. Weather can apply status effects — an icy locus chills you unless you're immune from a passive, gear, or an active buff.
  • Tuned per zone. Indoor scenes stay lit; outdoor ones live and breathe with the clock and the sky.
Guilds & the Dragon Ball endgame

Hold all seven, and the world comes for you.

Guilds aren't just chat channels. They own real estate in the world, and the Dragon Ball endgame runs straight through them.

  • Guild bases. Your guild owns a physical house scene with pedestals, furniture, aura emitters, and a shared vault. Customisable. Loseable.
  • The Dragon Ball system. Exactly one of each of the seven exists in the entire world. Some sit at fixed spots; others lie dormant on a single boss until it drops.
  • PvP over the balls. The seven are the endgame. Guilds fight over them in PvP zones, and lifting one off a rival's pedestal is fair game.
  • Wishes. Hold all seven on your guild's pedestals continuously, and your guild banks a wish — then the balls scatter again.
  • Global broadcasts. Every pickup, drop, and pedestal placement fires a cross-scene alert. The whole world hears.
Build a home

Somewhere to come back to between fights

When the dust settles, you have a place. A house you own, with furniture you earned, in a corner of the world you chose.

  • Player owned houses. Buy a plot, walk inside, decorate. Yours across sessions, yours across patches, with an access list for who gets in.
  • Furniture. Bought from vendors, dropped from content, and unique pieces from bonus objectives, secrets, and the hardest clears. Some even emit auras.
  • Place it where you want. Free placement with grid and half-grid snapping. Rotate, layer, and build an interior that tells your story.
  • Crafting. Turn raw drops into furniture, consumables for the next run, and gear you can't buy anywhere.
  • Show off. Other players can visit. They'll see the Dragon Ball trophy you almost didn't get.

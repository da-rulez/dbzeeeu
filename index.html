The world of DBZee

An online action RPG with a heartbeat

Shader-driven transformation auras that light the world. Guild wars over months-long Dragon Ball collection. A living world built in Godot 4 with hand-tuned netcode.

Transformations

SSJ, MUI, and beyond — auras hug your silhouette and cast real Light2D onto the world. Step into a dark cave and the gold pours onto the dirt at your feet.

The Seven Balls

One of each ball exists in the entire world at any time. Hunt them across overworld coords or grind the right NPC for a 1-in-10,000 drop. Months-long endgame.

Guild Wars

Place balls on your guild's pedestals. Rival guilds can cast a 10-second steal. Hold all seven for a single second and your guild earns a wish.

Real-time Combat

Targeted casting, channels, interrupts, and PvP loot grace periods. The same systems that drive raids drive the open world.

Dynamic Lighting

Auras, embers, and outline coronas all emit light. Day-night cycle and per-scene tint route through one CanvasModulate path so it all composites cleanly.

Built in Godot

Open engine, native client, no launcher. Available on Windows, Linux, and macOS, with a browser build for trying the demo.
